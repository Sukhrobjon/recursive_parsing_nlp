1. Click the grey gear icon in the upper-right corner of the dbt Cloud window; this opens the account drop-down.  
2. Click “Account settings” in the drop-down panel to load the administrative console.  
3. Click “Projects” in the left-hand sidebar; the list of all projects appears in the main pane.  
4. Click the single project row labeled “DesktopEnv”; a slide-out panel containing the project details opens on the right.  
5. Click the green “Edit” button at the bottom of that slide-out panel to make the fields editable.  
6. Type OSWorld into the “Project name” input located under the “Overview” subtitle, replacing the old value.  
7. Click the green “Save” button at the bottom of the panel to submit the change.  
8. If a warning modal appears about potential effects, click the blue “Continue” button to confirm the rename operation.  
9. Observe that the heading in the slide-out now shows “OSWorld”; this confirms the update persisted.  
10. Refresh the browser (Ctrl+R or ⌘+R) and verify in the Projects list that the entry formerly named “DesktopEnv” now reads “OSWorld,” ensuring the change is visible across sessions.
