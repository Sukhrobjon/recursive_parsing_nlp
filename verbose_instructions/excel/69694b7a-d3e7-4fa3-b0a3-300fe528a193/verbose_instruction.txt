1. Click cell G1 in the Sales sheet.  
2. Type Revenue and press Enter to create the new header.  
3. Click cell G2.  
4. Type the lookup-based revenue formula exactly as shown below, then press Enter:  

```excel
=E2*VLOOKUP(C2,'Retail Price'!$A$2:$B$23,2,FALSE)*(1-F2)
```  
   • E2 = Quantity sold (or Units)  
   • C2 = Product name used to look up its retail price  
   • 'Retail Price'!$A$2:$B$23 = lookup table with Product in column A and Price in column B  
   • F2 = Discount percentage (e.g., 0.15 for 15 %)  

5. Hover over the small square (fill handle) at the bottom-right corner of G2 until the cursor becomes a black plus sign.  
6. Drag the fill handle down to the last data row (for example G36) to copy the formula for every transaction.  
7. Press Ctrl + S once to save the updated worksheet.  
8. Click any single cell inside the data range (A1:G36) so Excel knows what to include in the pivot table.  
9. Navigate to the Insert tab on the Ribbon and click PivotTable (first button on the left).  
10. Verify that “Select a table or range” is chosen and the Table/Range box shows $A$1:$G$36.  
11. Select New Worksheet, then click OK. Excel jumps to the new PivotTable sheet.  
12. Drag the field Web Site from the PivotTable Fields panel to the Rows area.  
13. Drag the field Web Site again from the field list into the Values area.  
14. Click the drop-down in the Values area (it currently reads “Sum of Web Site”), choose Value Field Settings…, select Count, and click OK.  
15. Drag the field Product (or Item) to the Filters area.  
16. Click the filter drop-down now visible above the pivot table, check only Boomerang, and click OK.  
    Note that this filter ensures we count only transactions where boomerangs were sold.  
17. Double-click the sheet tab name (probably “Sheet1”) created for the pivot, type Sheet2, and press Enter to rename it.  
18. Click any cell inside the pivot table again to make the PivotTable Analyze tab appear.  
19. Go to PivotTable Analyze ➜ PivotChart in the Ribbon.  
20. Choose Clustered Bar under the Bar category and click OK. Excel inserts the chart on Sheet2.  
21. Click the generic title on the chart, type Sales frequency by website, and press Enter.  
22. Click once on the legend and press Delete to remove it.  
23. Press Ctrl + S to save all changes.  
24. Review Sheet2: we should see a bar chart without a legend and a pivot table showing the count of boomerang transactions per website, confirming success.
