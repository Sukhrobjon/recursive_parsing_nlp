1. Click the gear-shaped Settings icon in the upper-right corner of the Metabase screen  
2. Select “Admin settings” from the drop-down menu  
3. Click the “People” tab in the horizontal navigation bar at the top of the Admin panel  
4. Click the “Groups” tab in the vertical sidebar on the left  
5. Click the blue “Create a group” button in the upper-right corner of the Groups page  
6. Type Project Users into the “Group name” input field that appears  
7. Click the blue “Add” button to save the new group  
8. Verify that a new row labelled “Project Users” now shows up in the list (this confirms the group was created)  
9. Click the “Project Users” row to open the group details page  
10. Click the “Add members” button in the upper-right corner of the group details page  
11. Type J in the search box; when the drop-down list appears, click John Wilson to select him  
12. Click the blue “Add” button to add the user to the group  
13. Confirm that “John Wilson” now appears in the Members list of the “Project Users” group (this verifies success)
