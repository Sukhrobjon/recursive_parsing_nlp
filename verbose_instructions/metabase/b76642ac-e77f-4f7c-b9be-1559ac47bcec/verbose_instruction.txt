1. Click the “New” button on the Metabase top-right toolbar.  
2. Click “Question” in the drop-down panel that appears.  
3. Click “Raw Data” > choose your database (e.g., “Sample Database”) > click the “Orders” table.  
4. Click the blue “Summarize” button in the upper-right of the editor.  
5. Under “Group by”, click “+ Add group”.  
6. Select “User ID” (or “User → Name” if you prefer readable names). We need one row per user.  
7. Under “Summarize by”, hover over the default “Count of rows”, click the trash-can icon to delete it (we’ll create two explicit counts instead).  
8. Click “+ Add metric” → choose “Count of rows”.  
   • Click the small pencil icon next to the metric label and type total_orders as the custom name, then press Enter.  
9. Click “+ Add metric” again → choose “Count of rows” once more.  
   • Click “Filter” (tiny funnel icon that appears beside the metric).  
   • In the filter pop-up, select the “Tax” column, keep the operator “is greater than”, and type 5 as the value.  
   • Click “Add filter”.  
   • Click the pencil icon beside this second metric and rename it orders_with_tax_gt_5, then press Enter.  
10. Click the blue “Done” button at the lower-right of the Summarize side panel.  
    • We now see two numeric columns: total_orders and orders_with_tax_gt_5 for each user.  
11. Click the “Custom column (fx)” button located just above the result table (to the right of “Summarize”).  
12. In the “Custom expression” editor that opens, type the formula exactly as below:  

```
[orders_with_tax_gt_5] / [total_orders]
```  

13. In the “Custom column name” field below the editor, type tax percentage.  
14. Click the “Formatting” drop-down, choose “Percentage”, and (optionally) set 1 decimal place.  
15. Click the blue “Done” button to add this new column.  
16. Verify that the result grid now shows three columns:  
    • user_id (or user name)  
    • total_orders  
    • tax percentage (values such as 0 %, 33.3 %, 100 %)  
    Confirm that users with every order over \$5 in tax show 100 %, while users with none show 0 %.  
17. Since “You don’t need to save it,” simply leave the browser tab open or click “Close” when finished.
