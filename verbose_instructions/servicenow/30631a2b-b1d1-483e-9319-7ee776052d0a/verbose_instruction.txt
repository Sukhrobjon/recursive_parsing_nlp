1. Click the sidebar item labeled “Hardware” and wait until the catalog page finishes loading (you should see a list of physical devices).  
2. Click the search box in the upper-right corner that displays the placeholder text “Search catalog”.  
3. Type Standard Laptop and press Enter so the page filters to matching items; we should now see a single card or row titled “Standard Laptop”.  
4. Click the “Standard Laptop” card to open its configuration panel.  
5. Click each checkbox under the “Optional Software” section for Adobe Acrobat and Adobe Photoshop (both boxes must show a checkmark).  
6. Click the wide multiline input box next to “Additional software requirements”.  
7. Type Slack, Salesforce, QuickBooks, Google Workspace, Asana, Adobe Creative Cloud exactly as shown, then click outside the box so the text is saved.  
8. Switch attention to the summary panel on the right; click the Quantity dropdown and select 3.  
9. Verify that the summary now shows Quantity: 3 and lists Adobe Acrobat, Adobe Photoshop, and all additional software entries.  
10. Click the blue “Order Now” button at the bottom-right corner of the screen.  
11. Wait until the confirmation toast or dialog appears with a message like “Your order has been placed”; this indicates the request was successfully submitted.
