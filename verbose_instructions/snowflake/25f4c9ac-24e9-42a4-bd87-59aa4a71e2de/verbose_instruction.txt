1. Click the “Code Versions” button located in the upper-right corner of the worksheet window.  
2. Wait for the side panel titled “Code Versions” to slide in from the right; then click the second item in the list (it should be labeled with the second-newest timestamp).  
3. Observe that the SQL you need appears in a read-only area inside the side panel. Drag your cursor from the very first character to the last to highlight the entire statement set.  
4. Press Ctrl + C (Cmd + C on macOS) to copy the highlighted SQL to your clipboard.  
5. Click the gray “Close” button at the bottom-right corner of the “Code Versions” panel to collapse the side panel and return focus to the main worksheet.  
6. Click anywhere inside the worksheet editor, then press Ctrl + A (Cmd + A) to select all current text.  
7. Press Ctrl + V (Cmd + V) to paste the SQL you just copied, replacing the previous contents.  
8. Verify that the worksheet now shows the exact SQL text from version 2 (you can quickly compare the first and last lines with what you saw in the side panel).  
9. Leave the webpage open—do not click the browser’s close button or any navigation links—so the rolled-back SQL remains in place for further edits or execution.
