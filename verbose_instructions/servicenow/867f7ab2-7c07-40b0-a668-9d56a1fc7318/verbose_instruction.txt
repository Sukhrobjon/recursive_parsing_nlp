1. Click the “Incidents” application tab on the left-hand navigation pane to load the incidents list view.  
2. Confirm the list tab title reads “Incidents > All” so we know we are looking at every record.  
3. Click the funnel icon (middle icon under the page title bar) to open the Filter and Sort panel; wait until the panel expands below the list header.  
4. Click the blue button labeled “Add Sort” on the first row of the panel; a new row labeled “Order results by the following fields” appears.  
5. Click the empty dropdown field inside this new row. Type “Caller” in the search box, then click the option “Caller” when it appears; the field now displays “Caller”.  
6. Click the dropdown immediately to the right of “Caller” that currently shows “z to a”; in the menu, click “a to z” so the sort order is ascending.  
7. Click the blue “Run” button at the far right of the panel to execute the sort.  
8. Wait for the list to refresh; verify the Caller column now shows names in alphabetical order from A to Z (e.g., “Aaron Smith” appears before “Barbara Cho”).
