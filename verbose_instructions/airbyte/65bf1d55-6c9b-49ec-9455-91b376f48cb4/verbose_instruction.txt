4) Click the gray “Settings” tab in the secondary left-hand panel  
5) Scroll to the very bottom of the Settings page and click the red “Delete connection” link  
6) Type DELETE in the confirmation modal’s text box to acknowledge permanent removal  
7) Click the red “Delete” button inside the modal; the UI should redirect back to the Connections list

11) Click the kebab menu (three vertical dots) at the far right of that row  
12) Choose “Delete source” from the dropdown menu  
13) In the confirmation dialog type DELETE and click the “Delete” button  
14) Observe a green toast “Source deleted” and confirm the source list no longer shows “Cloud File” by searching for it  

15) Click the “Destinations” icon (arrow into box) on the left navigation bar  
16) Find the destination named “Snowflake” (used only by the removed connection)  
17) Click the kebab menu on that row and select “Delete destination”  
18) Enter DELETE in the modal and press the red “Delete” button  
19) Wait for the success toast; verify by searching “Snowflake” in the destination list—no matching row should appear  

20) Refresh the browser with Ctrl+R and quickly check all three sections (Connections, Sources, Destinations) to ensure none of the deleted objects re-appear; the cleanup is complete
