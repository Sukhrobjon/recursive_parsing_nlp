1. Click the blue “+” button located in the upper-right corner of the Snowsight window  
2. Choose “Folder” from the drop-down panel that appears  
3. Type Online shopping into the Name input box that pops up  
4. Click the Create Folder button at the lower-right of the dialog; we will see the new folder listed in the left-side navigation tree  
5. Click the blue “+” button again in the upper-right corner  
6. Select “SQL Worksheet” from the menu; a new tab called Worksheet X will open in the main editor pane  
7. Hover your mouse over the Worksheet X tab title until the three-dot “⋮” icon becomes visible, then click the icon  
8. Click Rename in the drop-down list  
9. Type Books products and press Enter; the tab now shows the new name  
10. Click the “✕” on the Books products tab to save context and close the worksheet (the worksheet is automatically saved in the root for now)  
11. Locate the Online shopping folder in the left navigation panel and click it once so the folder becomes highlighted; we are now “inside” that folder  
12. Click the blue “+” button in the upper-right corner again  
13. Select “SQL Worksheet” from the drop-down; a new Worksheet Y tab opens under the Online shopping folder context  
14. Hover over the Worksheet Y tab, click the revealed “⋮” icon, and choose Rename  
15. Type Electronics products and press Enter; the tab title updates accordingly  
16. Click the “✕” on the Electronics products tab to close it; Snowflake auto-saves the worksheet inside the Online shopping folder  
17. Verify the result: expand the Online shopping folder in the left navigation pane and confirm both worksheets—Books products and Electronics products—are listed underneath.
