1. Click the “New” button in the top-right corner of the Metabase window.  
2. Choose “Question” from the drop-down panel that appears.  
3. In the database picker modal, select “Sample Database,” then click the “Orders” table name.  
4. Click the blue “Visualize” button at the bottom-right of the data preview.  
5. Click the “Summarize” button (top-right, next to the filter icon).  
6. Under “Summarize by” keep the default “Count” metric (this counts all rows).  
7. Under “Group by,” click “+ Add grouping,” then choose “Total.”  
8. Click the “Auto binned” pill that appears under the “Total” column. A small pop-up with preset bin sizes opens.  
9. Select a bin size, watch the preview chart, and repeat with other presets until the y-axis (vertical axis) max label is close to 1,400 (≈1.4K). Typical choices are “10,” “20,” “25,” and “50”; pick whichever yields a maximum bar height around 1,400.  
   • Verification: when you hover a tallest bar, the tooltip should read ~1,350–1,450—confirming the range is correct.  
10. Click the blue “Done” button in the summarization sidebar. Metabase now shows a default bar chart.  
11. Click the “Visualization” selector (bottom-left) and choose “Bar” if it is not already selected (Metabase uses the Bar visualization for histograms).  
12. Click the Bar visualization thumbnail again to open the “Bar Options” side panel.  
13. Expand the “Display” accordion.  
14. Toggle “Show values on data points” to ON. A sub-option appears.  
15. In “Values to show,” choose “All.” All bars now show their counts.  
16. Under “Number formatting,” open the “Auto formatting” drop-down and choose “Compact.” Bar labels switch from “1,432” to “1.4K,” matching compact style.  
17. Click “Done” at the bottom of the Bar Options panel to save the style changes.  
18. Verification: glance at the y-axis—its top tick should be ≈1.4K, and each bar displays a compact value label (e.g., 1.1K).  
19. Click the cloud-shaped “Download full results” button in the lower-right corner of the page.  
20. In the download modal, select the “.png” option.  
21. Metabase downloads the file to your default folder (usually /home/user/Downloads).  
22. Open your file browser, navigate to /home/user/Downloads, locate the newly saved PNG (the name starts with the question title).  
23. Right-click the file, choose “Rename,” and type exactly: histogram.png  
24. Press Enter to confirm the rename. The visualization is now saved as histogram.png and ready for use.
