1. Click the “New” button in the top-right corner of the Metabase header.  
2. Select “Question” from the drop-down menu that appears.  
3. Click “Sample Database,” then choose the “Orders” table from the list.  
4. Click the blue “Pick the metric you want to see” button in the editing pane.  
5. Select “Average of …” from the aggregation options.  
6. Choose the “Total” field in the column list so the metric now reads “Average of Total.”  
7. Click the gray “Pick a column to group by” button directly beneath the metric.  
8. Select “User ID” to group the averages by each user.  
9. Click the “Sort” button (down-arrow icon) in the top bar of the editor.  
10. In the “Sort by” pop-up, choose “Average of Total” so the results are ordered by the calculated average.  
11. Click the “+” (plus) icon to the right of “Average of Total” in the sort dialog.  
12. Choose “User ID” so a secondary alphabetical sort is added after the primary numeric sort.  
13. Click the “Row limit” link that appears under the table preview.  
14. Type 10 in the input box and press Enter to limit the result set to the top 10 rows.  
15. Verify that the preview table now shows exactly 10 rows, grouped by “User ID,” sorted by “Average of Total,” and matching the expected output of the SQL query.
