1. Open your browser and log in to Apache Superset with a user that has database write permissions.  
2. Click “Data” in the top navigation bar.  
3. Click “Upload a CSV” from the drop-down.  
4. In the “Upload CSV to Database” modal, click the button labeled “Browse” (or “Choose File”).  
5. In the file-picker dialog that appears, navigate to /home/user on your local computer, select tutorial_flights.csv, and click “Open”.  
6. In the modal’s “Table Name” field, type  
   ```
   tutorial_flights
   ```  
7. In the “Database” drop-down, choose “MyDatabase”.  
8. Scroll to the “Parse Dates” section, click the input, and type exactly  
   ```
   Travel Date
   ```  
   (This tells Superset to convert the “Travel Date” column to DATE type.)  
9. Confirm that the “Delimiter” remains as “,” and that “Header Row” is checked (so the first line is treated as column names).  
10. Click “Save” at the bottom-right of the modal. Superset shows a brief spinner; when it finishes, we should see the message “Successfully created table tutorial_flights in database MyDatabase”.  
11. Click the blue link “tutorial_flights” in that success message to open the new dataset.  
12. Verify the schema: in the left sidebar, make sure the “Travel Date” column shows type “DATE”.  
13. Click the “SQL” tab in the upper-right of the dataset screen.  
14. Type the following validation query:  
    ```sql
    SELECT "Travel Date", * 
    FROM tutorial_flights
    LIMIT 10;
    ```  
15. Press the green “Run” button. Confirm that the first column displays ordinary date values (YYYY-MM-DD).  
16. If the dates look correct, click “Save Dataset” in the upper-right to persist metadata, then inform your leader the table is ready in MyDatabase.
