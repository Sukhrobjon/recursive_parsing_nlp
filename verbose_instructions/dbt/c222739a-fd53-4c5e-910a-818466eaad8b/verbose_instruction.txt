1. Click the Deploy button at the top navigation bar of the console  
2. Select Jobs from the drop-down menu that appears  
3. Click the blue Create job button on the Jobs page  
4. Choose Deploy job in the job-type selector that pops up  
5. Type Thanksgiving_Day into the Job name input under the Job settings section (make sure no extra spaces)  
6. Locate the Triggers section just below; toggle the Run on schedule switch so it turns blue (enabled)  
7. Click the Timing drop-down that now appears and select Cron schedule from the list  
8. Click into the Custom cron schedule (UTC) textbox that becomes visible and type the cron expression exactly as shown:  
   ```
   0 12 22-28 11 4
   ```  
   Note that:  
   • 0 = minute 0  
   • 12 = hour 12 (noon UTC)  
   • 22-28 = possible calendar dates of Thanksgiving week  
   • 11 = month November  
   • 4 = day-of-week Thursday  
   This combination fires only at 12:00 PM UTC on the Thursday that falls between 22–28 November, matching U.S. Thanksgiving Day.  
9. Verify that the Preview next runs panel (if available) shows the next Thanksgiving date at 12:00 UTC—confirming the cron is correct  
10. Scroll up (or simply look to the header) and click the Save button on the top right corner of the configuration page  
11. Wait for the green toast notification Job saved successfully to confirm the new job has been persisted under the Thanksgiving_Sales environment  
12. Navigate back to the Jobs list, find the row labeled Thanksgiving_Day, and check that its Schedule column displays Cron: 0 12 22-28 11 4 indicating the job is now scheduled correctly
