1. Navigate to the Superset web-UI in your browser and wait until the main landing page finishes loading.  
2. Click the “Charts” menu entry in the blue top navigation bar—we land on the Charts list view.  
3. Click the green “+ CHART” button in the upper-right corner to start the new-chart workflow.  
4. Click the grey “Choose a dataset” drop-down field in the modal that appears.  
5. Select the dataset named “flights” from the list (type flights in the search box if you do not see it immediately).  
6. Scroll the “Visualization Type” gallery on the right, single-click “Pivot Table”, and confirm the card is highlighted in blue.  
7. Click the blue “CREATE NEW CHART” button in the bottom-right of the modal—Superset now opens the chart builder.  
8. Drag the column “department” from the “Columns & Metrics” side panel into the “COLUMNS” pill area; release when the blue highlight shows.  
9. Drag the column “Travel Class” into the same “COLUMNS” pill area—it automatically nests after department.  
10. Drag the column “Travel Date” into the “COLUMNS” pill area so it becomes the third level of hierarchy (department ▸ travel class ▸ Travel Date).  
11. Click the grey “TIME GRAIN” drop-down directly beneath “Travel Date” in the control panel.  
12. Select “Month” so each Travel Date entry is truncated to the month bucket—we will see “MONTH” appear under the Travel Date pill.  
13. Drag the numeric column “Cost” to the “METRICS” pill area on the right side; Superset instantly wraps it in a SUM( Cost ) aggregate.  
14. Verify the “Rows” pill area is empty—this ensures all grouping is done on the column side only.  
15. Click the small blue floppy-disk icon that pops up near the top of the control panel (it appears once we make changes) to persist interim edits.  
16. Locate the “FILTERS” section just below the pill areas and click the “+ ADD FILTER” link.  
17. Choose “Travel Date” from the field drop-down that appears inside the new filter row.  
18. Click the grey “TIME RANGE” input inside that same filter row to open the date-range dialog.  
19. Click the “RANGE TYPE” selector in the upper-left of the dialog and choose “Custom”.  
20. Click the “START (INCLUSIVE)” calendar field, switch to “Specific Date/Time” (bottom tab), set “2011-01-01 00:00:00”, then press the blue “Ok” button.  
21. Click the “END (INCLUSIVE)” calendar field, again switch to “Specific Date/Time”, set “2011-06-30 00:00:00”, and press “Ok”.  
22. Click the blue “APPLY” button in the lower-right of the date-range dialog—notice the filter pill now shows the precise range.  
23. Click the large blue “RUN” button in the upper-left of the chart canvas; Superset queries the backend and renders a pivot table whose columns read Department ▸ Travel Class ▸ 2011-01, 2011-02 … 2011-06 and whose cell values are sums of cost.  
24. Confirm that each column header is a three-level breadcrumb (hover to see full path) and that no months outside Jan-Jun 2011 are present—this validates our grouping and time filter.  
25. Click the purple “SAVE” button in the top-right corner of the window to open the Save Chart dialog.  
26. Type “TravelPrivot” (exact spelling) into the “CHART NAME” input field.  
27. Ensure “Save as new” is selected (radio button) so we do not overwrite any existing chart.  
28. Click the blue “SAVE” button at the bottom of the dialog—Superset writes the chart definition to metadata.  
29. Observe the green toast notification “Chart TravelPrivot was saved successfully” in the lower-right corner—this is our success confirmation.  
30. Click the left-facing arrow next to “Charts” in the breadcrumb bar to return to the Charts list; type TravelPrivot into the search box and verify the row appears with Visualization Type “Pivot Table” and last modified timestamp matching the current time.
