1. Click the “+” (Add New Sheet) tab at the bottom of the workbook.  
2. Click the new tab that appears (it will be named “Sheet2” by default) to make it active.  
3. Click cell A1, type Year, and press Tab.  
4. Type CA changes in cell B1, press Tab.  
5. Type FA changes in cell C1, press Tab.  
6. Type OA changes in cell D1, press Enter.  
7. Click cell A2 and type 2015.  
8. Press Enter, then type 2016 in cell A3.  
9. Continue pressing Enter and typing 2017, 2018, 2019 in cells A4, A5, A6 respectively.  
10. Click cell B2.  
11. Type =( in the formula bar.  
12. Switch to the Sheet1 tab.  
13. Click cell B3.  
14. Type - (a minus sign).  
15. Click cell B2 in Sheet1.  
16. Type )/ (close parenthesis and a forward slash).  
17. Click cell B2 in Sheet1 again.  
18. Press Enter to complete the formula.  
19. Switch back to Sheet2.  
20. Click cell B2, then click the % icon on the toolbar to format it as a percentage.  
21. Move the cursor to the lower-right corner of cell B2 until it changes to a small black plus (fill handle).  
22. Drag the fill handle down to cell B6 to copy the percentage formula for all years in the CA changes column.  
23. Click cell C2.  
24. Type =( in the formula bar.  
25. Go to Sheet1 and click cell C3.  
26. Type - (minus sign).  
27. Click cell C2 in Sheet1.  
28. Type )/ (close parenthesis and slash).  
29. Click cell C2 in Sheet1 again.  
30. Press Enter.  
31. Return to Sheet2, click cell C2, and click the % icon on the toolbar.  
32. Drag the fill handle from cell C2 down to C6 to fill the FA changes column.  
33. Click cell D2.  
34. Type =( in the formula bar.  
35. Switch to Sheet1 and click cell D3.  
36. Type - (minus sign).  
37. Click cell D2 in Sheet1.  
38. Type )/ (close parenthesis and slash).  
39. Click cell D2 in Sheet1 again.  
40. Press Enter.  
41. Switch back to Sheet2, select cell D2, and click the % button on the toolbar.  
42. Drag the fill handle from cell D2 down to D6 to populate the OA changes column.  
43. Review cells B2:D6 to ensure all values display percentage signs and the formulas reference the correct rows.
