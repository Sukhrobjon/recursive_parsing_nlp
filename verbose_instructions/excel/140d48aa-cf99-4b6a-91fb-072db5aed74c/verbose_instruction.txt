1. Click cell D1 in the open workbook “WeeklySales.xlsx”.  
2. Type Week Sales COGS Summary into the formula bar directly under the ribbon, then press Enter to commit the header.  
3. Click cell D2 (first data row of the new column).  
4. Type the following concatenation formula into the formula bar:  
```excel
=$A$1 & ": " & $A2 & ", " & $B$1 & ": " & $B2 & ", " & $C$1 & ": " & $C2
```  
   • Note that we use absolute references ($A$1, $B$1, $C$1) for the header cells so the text labels stay fixed, and relative references (A2, B2, C2) for the row values so they update as we copy the formula downward.  
5. Press Enter; cell D2 should now display something like “Week: 1, Sales: 12500, COGS: 9300” (values will vary based on your sheet).  
6. Hover over the small square (fill handle) in the bottom-right corner of cell D2 until the cursor changes to a black plus sign.  
7. Click-and-drag the fill handle straight down to cell D11 (or the last data row) to copy the formula for all rows. Release the mouse—each row in column D should now contain its own Week-Sales-COGS summary string.  
8. Scan a few random cells in column D to verify the pattern matches “Week: [Week Value], Sales: [Sales Value], COGS: [COGS Value]” for every row; correct any anomalies if needed.  
9. Press Ctrl+S (or click the Save icon in the upper-left) to save the updated workbook.
