1. Expand your Google Cloud project in the BigQuery Explorer panel by clicking the small gray triangle to the left of the project name.  
2. Scroll to the bottom of the object list and locate the section labeled “Saved Charts.”  
3. Click the gray triangle beside “Saved Charts” to reveal any existing charts.  
4. Hover over the chart named Mychart; a vertical three-dot (︙) icon appears on the right.  
5. Click the three-dot (︙) icon to open the context menu.  
6. Select “Delete” from the dropdown list; a confirmation dialog titled “Delete chart Mychart?” pops up.  
7. Read the confirmation text, then click the red “DELETE” button in the dialog.  
8. Verify the deletion: ensure Mychart no longer appears under “Saved Charts,” and look for a green toast message in the lower-right corner that reads “Chart deleted.”
