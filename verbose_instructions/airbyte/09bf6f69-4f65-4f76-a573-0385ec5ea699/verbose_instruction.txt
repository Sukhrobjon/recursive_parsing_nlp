1) Open the browser tab that already has Airbyte running (http://localhost:8000 by default).  
2) Click the menu item “Connections” in the dark-blue left sidebar to display the list of existing connections.  
3) Locate the required connection row (for example “postgres_to_bigquery”). If the list is long, type part of the name into the search bar at the top-right of the Connections page, then press Enter to filter.  
4) Click the connection name hyperlink itself (not the three-dot menu) so we enter the connection’s detail page.  
5) Press the “Sync History” tab just under the connection title; we can now see a table of past sync runs.  
6) Look at the first row in the table (Status column should read “SUCCEEDED” or “FAILED” with the most recent timestamp).  
7) Click the blue text “See logs” in that first row; a side panel titled “Logs” opens on the right with the full stdout / stderr output.  
8) Scroll to the top of this Logs panel and click the gray button “Download logs” (cloud-arrow icon). The browser download dialog appears.  
9) Change the “File name” field to exactly: airbyte_log.txt  
10) In the location tree on the left side of the dialog, click “Desktop” once so it is highlighted as the save destination.  
11) Click the blue “Save” button. The dialog closes and the file starts downloading to Desktop.  
12) Wait until the browser download notification says “airbyte_log.txt – Complete”.  
13) Minimize the browser, then double-click the “Desktop” shortcut/folder to open it and visually confirm that airbyte_log.txt is present.  
14) Double-click airbyte_log.txt to open it with the default text editor; verify that the first few lines show recognizable Airbyte log content (e.g., “INFO i.a.w.t.BaseWorker Started worker” ).  
15) Close the text editor—our sync log is now saved and validated on the Desktop.
