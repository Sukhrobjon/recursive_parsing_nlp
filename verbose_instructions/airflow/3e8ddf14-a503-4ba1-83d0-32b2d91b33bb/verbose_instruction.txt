1. Switch to “Chromium” on the left panel to bring the Astronomer Airflow tab into focus  
2. Verify that the Airflow DAGs home page is displayed (you should see the table of DAGs with columns like “DAG”, “Schedule”, “Recent Tasks”)  
3. Move your cursor to the top-right corner of the page and click the circular user-avatar icon (it usually shows your initials); a small drop-down menu appears  
4. Click the “Log Out” (sometimes labelled “Sign Out”) option in that drop-down menu  
5. Wait a few seconds until the browser automatically redirects to the Airflow login screen showing two empty fields titled “Username” and “Password” with a “Sign In” button below them  
6. Confirm that the URL in the address bar ends with “/login”; this indicates the session has been terminated successfully  
7. (Optional) Close the browser tab by clicking the “X” on the current tab or pressing Ctrl+W to fully exit the Airflow interface  
8. You are now safely logged out; no further action is required
