1) Navigate to Google Cloud Console and click BigQuery from the left-hand navigation menu so the BigQuery web UI loads.  
2) Locate the Explorer panel on the left, hover over CONNECTIONS, and click the small grey triangle to expand the list.  
3) Find the external connection named PostgresData in the list and click PostgresData once so its Details panel opens on the right.  
4) Click the pencil-shaped Edit connection button that appears in the Details panel header.  
5) Scroll down in the Edit Connection dialog until you reach the Advanced settings section.  
6) Locate the JSON unfolding toggle switch; click the switch so it moves to the left and shows an inactive/grey state (OFF).  
7) Click the blue Save button at the bottom of the dialog to apply the change.  
8) Wait for the green confirmation banner that says “Connection updated” to appear; this verifies the setting was saved.  
9) Re-click PostgresData in the Explorer panel, review the Details panel, and confirm the JSON unfolding field now reads Disabled, ensuring the configuration change succeeded.
