1. Open your web browser and navigate to the company Hardware Store portal (e.g., https://hardware.company.com).  
2. Click the “Hardware” tab in the left-hand sidebar; wait until the catalog grid finishes loading (you should see a spinning loader disappear).  
3. In the top-right search bar labeled “Search catalog,” click once inside the field, type Loaner Laptop exactly, and press Enter; the page will refresh showing only matching items.  
4. Locate the product card titled “Loaner Laptop” (it has a silver laptop thumbnail). Click anywhere on that card to open the item detail page.  
5. In the configuration form that appears:  
   a. Click the text box under the label “When do you need it ?” and type By the end of the month.  
   b. Click the dropdown under “How long do you need it for ?”; from the list of durations, choose 1 day (a blue checkmark will confirm selection).  
6. Move to the panel on the right side titled “Order Summary.” Click the “Quantity” field, type 9, and press Enter; verify the subtotal updates to reflect nine units.  
7. Double-check that the summary now shows:  
   • Product: Loaner Laptop  
   • Quantity: 9  
   • When needed: By the end of the month  
   • Duration: 1 day  
8. Click the green “Order Now” button beneath the summary; a spinner will appear next to the button. Wait until the success toast “Order request submitted” pops up in the bottom-right corner.  
9. Confirm the request by clicking the “View Requests” link in the toast; on the Requests page, ensure a new row with status “Pending Approval” lists 9 × Loaner Laptop with the correct need-by date.
