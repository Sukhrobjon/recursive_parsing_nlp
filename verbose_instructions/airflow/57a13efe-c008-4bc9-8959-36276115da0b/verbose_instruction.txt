1. Open the Chromium browser window already running the Astronomer Airflow UI.  
2. Click the text box of the browser’s address bar, type your deployment URL (for example, http://localhost:8080), and press Enter to refresh the page.  
3. Locate workFlow_Echo in the DAGs list; if the list is long, type workFlow_Echo into the “Filter Dag” search bar at the top-left corner and watch the table shorten to one row.  
4. Verify that the “On/Off” toggle immediately to the left of workFlow_Echo is blue (ON).  
   • If it is grey (OFF), Click the toggle once to unpause the DAG so that manual triggers are allowed.  
5. Hover over the workFlow_Echo row and Click the DAG name itself (the blue hyperlink) to open the DAG details page.  
6. Switch to the Graph view by Clicking the “Graph” tab in the horizontal menu directly under the DAG name.  
7. Wait until the DAG graph canvas renders; you should see the task boxes connected by arrows (e.g., start → echo_task → end).  
8. Click the blue “Play” triangle icon (Trigger DAG) in the upper-right corner of the Graph view toolbar.  
9. In the “Trigger DAG” modal that appears, leave the default execution date and time, and Click the blue “Trigger” button.  
10. Watch the toast notification at the bottom-right corner; confirm the green “Created” message appears, indicating the run was submitted successfully.  
11. Observe the nodes in the graph: they will change from light grey to yellow (queued) and then to green (success) once each task finishes.  
12. Refresh the page by Clicking the circular arrow icon in the browser toolbar or pressing F5, and ensure the run counter in the top-left corner of the Graph view now shows at least 1 and the latest run is green.  
13. Click the “Runs” dropdown (it shows execution dates) and confirm today’s timestamp is present, verifying the DAG workFlow_Echo was triggered from Graph view successfully.
