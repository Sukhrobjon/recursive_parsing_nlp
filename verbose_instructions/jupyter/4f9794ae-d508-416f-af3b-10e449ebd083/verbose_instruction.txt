1) Locate the tab or browser window that already displays your target notebook (filename should end in “.ipynb”).  
2) Confirm the notebook is in “Edit” mode—verify that the grey toolbar strip showing “File”, “Edit”, “View”, “Run”, “Kernel”, etc. is visible.  
3) Click the “Run” menu item in that toolbar (it is between “View” and “Kernel”).  
4) Select “Run All Cells” from the drop-down list that appears; the option label may read exactly “Run All Cells” or “Run All”.  
5) Observe the execution status: each cell’s bracket indicator on the left will switch from “[ ]” to “[*]” while running and finally to a number like “[12]” when finished.  
6) Wait until no brackets show “[*]” and the status line at the top right returns to “Kernel Idle”; this confirms every cell has completed.  
7) Scroll down the notebook to quickly scan outputs—look for expected tables, plots, or printed text to ensure results rendered correctly.  
8) If any cell shows red error text, click that cell once, then press “Shift + Enter” to re-run just that cell and read the traceback for details.  
9) Optionally save the successful run: press “Ctrl + S” (or click “File” → “Save and Checkpoint”) so the executed outputs persist.
