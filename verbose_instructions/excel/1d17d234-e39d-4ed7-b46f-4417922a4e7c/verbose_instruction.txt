1) Click the “+” (New Sheet) icon at the bottom of the Excel window to insert a blank worksheet.  
2) Double-click the new sheet tab (likely named “Sheet2” automatically); if it shows a different name, type Sheet2 and press ENTER to confirm.  
3) Click cell A1, keep the mouse button down, and drag right to cell C1 so that A1:C1 are highlighted.  
4) Click the Home tab ➜ press the “Merge & Center” button in the Alignment group; the three cells now act as one merged cell.  
5) Type Investment Summary into the merged cell and press ENTER; the text should appear centered across columns A-C.  
6) Click cell A2, hold and drag to B2 so that A2:B2 are selected.  
7) Click Home ➜ Merge & Center again to combine A2 and B2.  
8) Type High Interest Rate in the merged A2:B2 cell and press ENTER.  
9) Click cell C2, drag to D2 to highlight C2:D2.  
10) Click Home ➜ Merge & Center to merge these two cells.  
11) Type Low Interest Rate into the merged C2:D2 cell and press ENTER.  
12) Click any blank cell (e.g., E3) to deselect and visually verify:  
    • A1:C1 shows “Investment Summary”, centered.  
    • A2:B2 shows “High Interest Rate”.  
    • C2:D2 shows “Low Interest Rate”.  
13) Press Ctrl+S to save the workbook so the new sheet and merged headers are retained.
