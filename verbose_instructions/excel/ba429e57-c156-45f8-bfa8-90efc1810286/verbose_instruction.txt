1. Click cell C1.  
2. Type “Maturity Date” and press ENTER to create the header.  
3. Click cell C2.  
4. Type the formula `=A2+B2` and press ENTER so Excel adds the loan start date in column A to the term in column B.  
5. Click the small square “fill handle” at the bottom-right corner of cell C2 (the cursor turns into a black cross).  
6. Drag the fill handle straight down to cell C10, then release the mouse so the formula copies through all loan rows.  
7. Press Ctrl + S to save the workbook and keep the new “Maturity Date” column.
