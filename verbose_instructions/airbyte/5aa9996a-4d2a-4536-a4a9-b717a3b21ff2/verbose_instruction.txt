1) Open the Airbyte UI in your browser and wait until the dashboard finishes loading  
2) Click the left-hand menu item “Connections” so we see the list of all configured connections  
3) Locate the row whose Source is “HTTP File” and Destination is “Local JSON”; confirm the Status column shows the grey label “Disabled”  
4) Click anywhere on that specific connection row (not the toggle yet) to enter the connection detail page  
5) Find the blue toggle switch in the upper-right corner that currently shows “Disabled” with the slider on the left  
6) Click the toggle once; it should slide to the right and the label should change to “Enabled” with a green background  
7) Wait 2-3 seconds; verify that a green toast message “Connection enabled” appears in the lower-left corner  
8) Observe the Schedule section: the “Next sync” timestamp should now display a future time instead of “—” confirming the connection is active  
9) Click the “Connections” breadcrumb at the top to return to the list view  
10) Confirm the Status column for the “HTTP File → Local JSON” connection now shows a green “Active” badge, indicating the enablement was successful
