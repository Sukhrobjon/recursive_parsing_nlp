1. Click the “Hardware” panel in the main navigation bar and wait until the catalog page finishes loading (the spinning loader in the top-right corner disappears).  
2. Click the search bar labeled “Search catalog” in the upper-right corner, type Development Laptop (PC), and press Enter to filter the results.  
3. Locate the item card titled “Development Laptop (PC)” in the results grid; click anywhere on that card to open the product-detail page.  
4. Scroll to the “Configuration” section (it sits directly beneath the product photo).  
5. Click the radio button whose label starts with “250” under the question “What size solid state drive do you want?” to select the 250 GB SSD option.  
6. Click the drop-down field for “Please specify an operating system,” then choose Ubuntu from the list.  
7. Move to the right-hand summary panel labeled “Order Details,” click inside the “Quantity” input, type 10, and press Tab to confirm.  
8. Verify that the summary panel now shows Quantity = 10, OS = Ubuntu, and SSD = 250 GB.  
9. Click the green “Order Now” button at the bottom of the summary panel.  
10. Wait until the confirmation toast reading “Your order has been placed” appears in the lower-right corner; this confirms the request for 10 configured laptops was successfully submitted.
