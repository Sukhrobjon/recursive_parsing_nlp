1. Click the “Data Products” menu in the left navigation pane, then click “Marketplace” in the sub-menu that appears.  
2. Click into the search bar labeled “Search providers and data products” at the very top of the Marketplace window.  
3. Type `American` and press Enter.  
4. Scan the results list; click the card titled “American Community Survey, 2016” (it should show the U.S. Census Bureau as provider).  
5. Click the blue “Get” button in the upper-right corner of the listing details page.  
6. In the confirmation dialog, click the secondary blue “Get” button at the bottom-right.  
7. Wait several seconds until the success dialog appears, then click the “Done” button.  
8. Click the “Databases” icon (cylinder symbol) in the left navigation pane to verify the share was added; the new database will have the provider’s default name (e.g., `AMERICAN_COMMUNITY_SURVEY_2016`).  
9. Click the triangle ▶ to the left of the new database name to expand it, then click to expand the only schema (usually `PUBLIC`).  
10. Scroll down the object list and confirm the table `ACS-2016-5-E-INDUSTRY_01_TABLEKEY` is present; this verifies the correct dataset is available.  
11. Click the “SQL Worksheet” button (paper & pen icon) in the top-right of Snowsight to open a new worksheet.  
12. Type the following commands to set context and preview data:  

```sql
-- Use the provider’s default database and schema
USE DATABASE AMERICAN_COMMUNITY_SURVEY_2016;
USE SCHEMA PUBLIC;

-- Inspect the first 10 rows of the target table
SELECT *
FROM "ACS-2016-5-E-INDUSTRY_01_TABLEKEY"
LIMIT 10;
```  

13. Click the “► Run” button in the upper-right of the worksheet.  
14. Confirm the results grid returns 10 rows without error; this final check proves the Marketplace import and access are successful.
