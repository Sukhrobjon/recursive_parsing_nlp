1) Locate the BigQuery console tab that is already open in your browser so the current worksheet stays in view.  
2) Click the small downward-pointing arrow ( ▾ ) just to the right of the worksheet name in the gray bar above the query editor; this opens the worksheet’s drop-down menu.  
3) Click “Revision history…” in that drop-down. A Revision History panel will slide in on the right side of the screen showing a vertical list of saved versions.  
4) Read the list from top to bottom—BigQuery orders revisions chronologically, newest at the top.  
   Note that “Version 1” is the most recent save, so the second item in the list is the previous save we need.  
5) Click once on the second item (it will read something like “Version 2 – <timestamp>”) to highlight it; the SQL snapshot for that version appears in the preview pane below.  
6) Verify the preview looks correct (we can scroll inside the preview to skim the SQL).  
7) Click the blue button labeled “Restore this version” at the upper-right corner of the preview pane.  
8) In the confirmation dialog that pops up, click “Restore” again. The Revision History panel will close automatically, and the query editor now shows the SQL text from Version 2.  
9) Press Ctrl+S (or click the floppy-disk icon in the toolbar) to save the worksheet so Version 2 becomes the current head revision.  
10) Check the status bar at the bottom of the editor—if it flashes “Saved” we know the rollback was successful.  
11) Leave the browser tab open; do NOT click the ‘X’ on the tab or close the window. The worksheet is now rolled back to the second version and remains available for further edits or execution.
