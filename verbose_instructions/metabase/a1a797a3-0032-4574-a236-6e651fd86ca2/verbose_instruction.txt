1. Click the grey gear icon in the upper-right corner of the Metabase window  
2. Select “Admin settings” from the dropdown panel that appears  
3. Click the “People” tab in the horizontal menu at the top of the Admin panel  
4. Locate the user row for “John Wilson”; click the three vertical dots (…) at the far right of that row  
5. Choose “Reset password” from the context menu  
6. Click the blue “Reset password” button in the confirmation dialog  
7. Click the copy-to-clipboard icon (clipboard symbol) immediately to the right of the temporary password shown next to the “Show” button; Metabase copies the temp password to your clipboard (we will use it in a moment)  
8. Click the grey “Done” button to close the dialog  
9. Click “Exit admin” in the upper-right corner to return to the normal interface  
10. Click the gear icon again (upper-right)  
11. Select “Account settings” from the dropdown panel  
12. Click the “Password” tab in the center of the Account Settings page  
13. Paste the copied temporary password into the “Current password” input field (Ctrl/⌘+V)  
14. Type metabase123 in the “Create a password” input field  
15. Retype metabase123 in the “Confirm your password” input field  
16. Click the blue “Save” button; a green toast notification (“Your password has been updated”) should appear in the bottom-left corner, confirming the password reset was successful
