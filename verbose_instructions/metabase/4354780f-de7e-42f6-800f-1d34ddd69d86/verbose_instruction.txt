1. Click the “New” button in the upper-right corner of Metabase.  
2. Choose “Question” from the drop-down menu.  
3. Click “Sample Database,” then select the “Orders” table.  
4. Click the blue “Join data” button that appears just below the column list.  
5. In the pop-up, click “Pick table…”, scroll or type to find “Products,” and select it.  
6. In the “How do these tables connect?” prompt, leave the default “Orders → Product ID = Products → ID” join condition, then click “Done.” (Note: this uses the common key so we can pull the product information.)  
7. Click the green “Summarize” button (bar-chart icon).  
8. In the Summarize panel, under “Summarize by,” confirm “Count of rows” is present.  
9. Under “Group by,” click “+ Add,” expand “Products,” and select “Category.” (Metabase will show it as “Products → Category.”)  
10. Click “Done” to apply the aggregation.  
11. Click the “Sort” button (two-arrow icon) in the toolbar.  
12. In the Sort modal, choose “Count” as the field.  
13. Click the small triangle next to “Count” so it points downward, setting the order to Descending. Then click “Done.”  
14. Click the blue “Visualize” button (bottom-right) to open the chart options.  
15. In the Visualization sidebar, click “Funnel” to switch the chart type. Metabase now shows a funnel ordered by Product Category, largest count at the top.  
16. Review the chart quickly—confirm that the first segment has the highest count and the segments decrease downward (verifies descending sort).  
17. Click the “Download” (cloud) icon at the bottom-right of the screen.  
18. Select “.png” from the download format list and click “Download.”  
19. In your system’s file dialog, navigate to /home/user/Downloads (Metabase automatically saves there for most browsers).  
20. Rename the file to “funnel_chart.png” and click “Save.”  
21. Open the file to confirm it displays the funnel with descending Product Category counts (verification step).
