1. Click the small plus icon labeled “Append sheet” located just to the left of the Sheet1 tab; Excel creates a new worksheet named “Sheet2”.  
2. Confirm the tab text now reads “Sheet2” (this verifies the sheet was added).  
3. Click the “Sheet1” tab to return to the original data sheet.  
4. Drag across the column headers A through G so the entire data range A:G is highlighted.  
5. Click the top-menu path Insert ➜ PivotTable…; the “Create PivotTable” dialog opens with the selected range already filled in.  
6. Leave “Select a table or range” chosen and click the OK button; the PivotTable Field List appears on the right side.  
7. Drag the field named Product from the upper “Available Fields” list down into the “Rows” quadrant.  
8. Drag the field named Revenue ($) down into the “Values” quadrant; Excel should automatically change the value field setting to “Sum of Revenue ($)”.  
9. Click inside the PivotTable so the PivotTable Analyze tab is active, then click PivotTable Analyze ➜ PivotTable ➜ Move PivotTable.  
10. In the “Move PivotTable” dialog, choose “Existing Worksheet”.  
11. Click the location selector button (small grid icon), then click cell A1 on Sheet2 (you will automatically switch to Sheet2).  
12. Click the same grid icon again to commit the reference so the location box now reads Sheet2!$A$1, and then press OK; the first PivotTable appears beginning in A1 on Sheet2.  
13. Verify that Column A lists each distinct Product and Column B shows the corresponding Sum of Revenue ($).  
14. Switch back to Sheet1 by clicking its tab.  
15. Highlight columns A through G again to re-select the full data area.  
16. Click Insert ➜ PivotTable… a second time, then press OK in the dialog (range is already correct).  
17. Drag Sales Channel from “Available Fields” into the “Rows” quadrant.  
18. Drag Revenue ($) into the “Values” quadrant (it should again read Sum of Revenue ($)).  
19. Click PivotTable Analyze ➜ PivotTable ➜ Move PivotTable.  
20. Choose “Existing Worksheet”, click the selector button, and this time click cell D1 on Sheet2.  
21. Click the selector button again so the reference shows Sheet2!$D$1, then press OK; the second PivotTable is now positioned starting in D1 on Sheet2.  
22. Confirm that Column D lists each distinct Sales Channel and Column E displays the corresponding Sum of Revenue ($).  
23. Press Ctrl+S to save the workbook, ensuring both PivotTables are stored.
