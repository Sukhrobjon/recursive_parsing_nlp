1. Open your web browser and navigate to the company’s designated hardware-purchasing portal (type the URL for “hardware-store.company.com” and press Enter).  
2. Log in by typing your corporate email in the “Email” field, your SSO password in the “Password” field, and clicking the blue “SIGN IN” button.  
3. Wait for the dashboard to load, then click the search bar at the top labeled “Search products, SKUs, or keywords.”  
4. Type Developer Laptop (Mac) and press Enter; the product catalog will refresh with matching results.  
5. Locate the tile titled “Developer Laptop (Mac) – 16-inch, Apple M-series” and click the product name to open its configuration page.  
6. Click the “CONFIGURE / BUY” button on the right side of the product image to open the customisation wizard.  
7. In the “Quantity” field at the top-left of the wizard, highlight the default value “1”, type 3, and press Tab to confirm we are ordering three units.  
8. Scroll down to the “Software & Services” section and click the grey “Add / Edit” link to expand the software checklist.  
9. Check the box next to Additional software requirements (a free-text field will appear directly beneath).  
10. Click inside the new text box and type exactly:  
   ```
   Salesforce, QuickBooks, Zoom, Microsoft Office 365, Asana, Adobe Creative Cloud
   ```  
11. Find the line item “Adobe Acrobat” and verify its toggle is OFF (grey). If it is ON (blue), click the toggle once to turn it OFF.  
12. Locate “Adobe Photoshop” and click its toggle so it turns ON (blue) to indicate inclusion.  
13. Locate “Eclipse IDE” and ensure its toggle is OFF (grey). If it is blue, click once to switch it OFF.  
14. Review the remaining software options quickly to confirm no other undesired items are selected; leave everything else at default.  
15. Click the green “SAVE CONFIGURATION” button at the bottom-right of the wizard; a confirmation toast “Configuration saved” should appear.  
16. Click the blue “ADD TO CART” button; you should see the cart icon in the upper-right corner update to “3 items.”  
17. Click the cart icon to open the mini-cart, then click the “VIEW CART” button.  
18. Verify the following on the cart page:  
    a) Product name: Developer Laptop (Mac)  
    b) Quantity: 3  
    c) Sub-total matches three units (confirm pricing if required)  
    d) Software line shows “Adobe Photoshop” and “Additional software requirements: Salesforce, QuickBooks, Zoom, Microsoft Office 365, Asana, Adobe Creative Cloud”  
    e) No mention of Adobe Acrobat or Eclipse IDE  
19. Click the orange “PROCEED TO CHECKOUT” button.  
20. On the “Shipping Information” page, confirm the default office address is selected; if not, click the radio button next to “Head Office – Receiving Dock” and click “CONTINUE.”  
21. On the “Payment” page, ensure “Corporate PO” is pre-selected; type the internal cost-center code (e.g., DEV-601) into the “PO Number” field and click “CONTINUE.”  
22. Review the “Order Summary” page once more for quantity (3), software selections, and cost; scroll to the bottom and click the blue “PLACE ORDER” button.  
23. Wait for the confirmation screen to display “Order #XXXXXX has been placed successfully.” Write down or copy the order number for records.  
24. Click the “DOWNLOAD RECEIPT (PDF)” link on the confirmation screen; when prompted, save the file to your desktop as Developer_Mac_Order.pdf for finance reference.  
25. Open Slack (or your team’s communication tool), navigate to the #it-procurement channel, and paste a message:  
   ```
   Order #XXXXXX: 3 x Developer Laptop (Mac) placed with required software (Photoshop + listed apps, no Acrobat, no Eclipse). Receipt saved in Teams/Procurement > 2024 Orders folder.
   ```  
26. Drag-and-drop Developer_Mac_Order.pdf into the same Slack message and click the paper-plane “SEND” icon.  
27. Wait for the upload to finish, then confirm the message appears in #it-procurement; your hardware order is now complete.
