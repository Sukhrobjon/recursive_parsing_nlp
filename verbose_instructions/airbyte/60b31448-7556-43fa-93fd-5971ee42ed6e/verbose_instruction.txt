1. Open the Airbyte homepage in your browser.  
2. Click the blue button labeled “Create your first connection” located at the bottom center of the screen.  
3. In the “Select a source” panel that appears, click the row titled “Sample Data (Faker)” (you will see its logo of a smiley face next to it).  
4. In the “Select a destination” panel, click the row titled “Local SQLite” (identified by the SQLite dolphin icon).  
5. Wait while Airbyte fetches the source schema; a small spinner and the message “Fetching the latest schema” will display for a few seconds.  
6. When the Connection Configuration page loads, scroll straight to the bottom without changing any fields—everything remains on default values (Destination namespace, Stream sync modes, Schedule type, etc.).  
7. Click the teal “Set up connection” button at the bottom-right corner of the page.  
8. Observe the new Connection Overview page: the connection name appears at the top, a green “Active” status pill is visible, and a “Sync now” button is enabled. This confirms the connection was created successfully.
