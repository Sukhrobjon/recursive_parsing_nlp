1) Click cell J2 in “Sheet1”.  
2) Type  =SUM(B2:D2)-SUM(F2:H2)  into the Formula Bar (the long white bar directly under the Ribbon).  
3) Press ENTER; Excel should now display a number (the gross profit for row 2).  
4) Double-click the small square “fill handle” at the bottom-right corner of cell J2; Excel automatically fills the formula down to J10 (rows 2-10).  
5) Scroll down and visually confirm that cells J2 through J10 all contain numerical values and no “#VALUE!” errors.

6) Click the white plus-sign icon labelled “+” (Insert Worksheet) to the right of the existing sheet tabs; Excel creates a new worksheet named “Sheet2”.  
7) Double-click the new tab text “Sheet2”, type  Sheet2  (or any name you prefer), and press ENTER.

8) Click cell A1 in “Sheet2”.  
9) Type  Year  and press TAB.  
10) Type  Gross Profit  in cell B1 and press ENTER; this sets up the two column headers.

11) Switch to “Sheet1” by clicking its tab.  
12) Drag to select cells A2 through A10 (the Year values).  
13) Press CTRL+C (or right-click and choose Copy).  
14) Switch back to “Sheet2”.  
15) Click cell A2.  
16) Press CTRL+V to paste; the years now appear in cells A2:A10.

17) Switch again to “Sheet1”.  
18) Drag to select cells J2 through J10 (the gross-profit numbers you calculated).  
19) Press CTRL+C.

20) Return to “Sheet2”.  
21) Right-click cell B2 and choose Paste Special → Values (or, in Excel 365, click the arrow under the Paste button and choose the clipboard icon labelled “Values”); this pastes only the numeric results—not the formulas—into B2:B10.

22) Check one random row (for example, row 5): confirm that the year in A5 matches the gross-profit figure in B5 exactly as it did in “Sheet1”; this verifies a correct copy.

23) Press CTRL+S.  
24) In the “Save As” dialog, choose the file type “Excel Workbook (*.xlsx)” to ensure compatibility with Excel 2007-365, pick a folder location, name the file, and click Save.

The new worksheet now contains the Year column in A and its corresponding Gross Profit in B, ready for further analysis.
