1. Click the “New” button in the Metabase top-right toolbar.  
2. Choose “SQL query” from the drop-down panel (this opens the SQL editor).  
3. Switch to the file viewer/editor on your computer and open question1.sql (double-click the file).  
4. Select all text in question1.sql (Ctrl + A) and copy it (Ctrl + C).  
5. Switch back to the Metabase browser tab.  
6. Click inside the large SQL input pane and paste the copied text (Ctrl + V).  
7. Click the blue “Run query” button on the right-hand side of the editor.  
8. Verify that the results grid below populates with rows (confirm there are no red error banners).  
9. Click the “Save” button in the top-right corner of the editor.  
10. Type question1 in the “Name” field of the save dialog.  
11. Leave “Collection” set to the default or choose the desired collection, then click the blue “Save” button.  
12. When the prompt “Add this to a dashboard?” appears, click “Yes, please!”.  
13. In the next dialog, click the button labeled “Create a new dashboard”.  
14. Type New Dashboard in the “Name” field and click “Create”.  
15. Confirm that the preview of question1 now appears on a blank dashboard canvas.  
16. Click “Save” in the upper-right corner of the dashboard screen to persist the dashboard.  
17. Verify success: the page title shows “New Dashboard”, and the tile underneath is labeled question1 and displays query results.
