1. Click the “New” button in the Metabase top-right toolbar.  
2. Choose “Question” from the drop-down panel that appears.  
3. Click “Sample Database” in the Choose a database dialog, then select the “Products” table.  
4. Click the blue “Summarize” button near the top-right of the editor.  
5. Click the grey “Pick the metric you want to see” pill.  
6. Scroll or type to find “Sum of …”, then click it.  
7. In the subsequent list, click “Price”. (Metabase now shows “Sum of Price”).  
8. After the word “by”, click the grey “Pick a column to group by” pill.  
9. Select “Category”. The phrase changes to “… by Category”.  
10. Click the blue “+” icon to the right of “Category” to add another grouping.  
11. Choose “Created At → Quarter”. We now have “… by Category and Created At – Quarter”.  
12. Click the blue “Visualize” button. Metabase shows a preview (usually a table).  
13. Click the “Visualization” selector at the bottom (currently “Table”) and choose “Bar”.  
14. Click “Bar” again to open the Bar Options side panel.  
15. In the Bar Options pane, expand “Display”.  
16. Under “Stacking”, click “Stack”. (Bars now appear stacked by Category within each quarter).  
17. Click “Done” at the bottom of the options pane to apply changes.  
18. Verify the result: we should see quarterly bars on the X-axis, height representing Sum of Price, and colors representing each Product Category.  
19. Click the cloud-shaped “Download” button at the bottom-right corner of the window.  
20. Choose “PNG” in the download format panel. Metabase immediately downloads a file to your browser’s default folder (usually ~/Downloads).  
21. Open your file browser and navigate to “/home/user/Downloads”.  
22. Right-click the newly downloaded PNG (name starts with the question title) and choose “Rename”.  
23. Type stack_chart.png and press Enter to confirm.  
24. Double-click stack_chart.png to open it and confirm the stacked bar chart displays correctly (height, colors, quarter labels).
