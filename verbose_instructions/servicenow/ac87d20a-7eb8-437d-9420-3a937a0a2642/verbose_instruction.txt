1. Scroll through the already-opened text file viewer until you see a line that matches the pattern  
   `User ID: <alphanumeric_id>`.  
   (We will call the value after the colon `<user_id>`.)  
   First, drag to highlight just the <user_id> portion, then press Ctrl + C to copy it to the clipboard— we’ll paste it in the form next.

2. Switch to the Chromium window showing the ServiceNow “User – New Record” form by clicking its icon on the left dock.

3. Click the empty input box labeled “User ID”, press Ctrl + V to paste the <user_id>, and press Tab to move to the next field (this confirms the value is registered).

4. Type “Robyn” in the “First name” field that is now focused, then press Tab once more.

5. Type “Christophel” in the “Last name” field that just received focus.

6. Click the large free-text field labeled “Title” (middle of the left column), type  
   `surquidry tassie Physostegia snootiness heterotopous`, and press Tab.

7. Locate the checkbox labeled “Locked out” directly beneath the Title field; if a blue/checked icon is present, click it once so the box becomes empty (unchecked).  
   Note that leaving this unchecked sets “Locked out” to false, as required.

8. Move to the right column and click inside the “Email” field; type  
   `robyn.christophel@example.com`.

9. Click the drop-down immediately under “Email” labeled “Language”; in the list that opens, scroll (or type “Eng”) and click the entry “English”.  
   We will see the chip “English” appear in the box.

10. Click the “Calendar integration” drop-down (directly below Language). From the options, choose “Outlook”.

11. Click the “Time zone” selector; in the pop-up list, scroll until you see “GMT” and click it once.  
    The field should now read “GMT”.

12. Click the “Date format” selector a bit lower; in the list, locate and click “dd-MM-yyyy”.

13. Click the “Business phone” field in the right column and type `(514) 386-6086`, then press Tab.

14. Type `(514) 703-5913` in the “Mobile phone” field that just gained focus.

15. Re-read all fields quickly to verify:
    • User ID shows the copied <user_id>  
    • First name Robyn, Last name Christophel  
    • Locked out checkbox is NOT ticked  
    • Title has the 6-word phrase  
    • Email, Language English, Calendar Outlook, Time zone GMT, Date format dd-MM-yyyy  
    • Business and Mobile phone numbers are correct.

16. Click the blue “Submit” button in the top-right corner of the form.

17. Wait for the page to reload and return to the User list. If no red validation banner appears, the record saved successfully. You can confirm by typing the <user_id> into the list filter search bar— the new Robyn Christophel entry should appear.
