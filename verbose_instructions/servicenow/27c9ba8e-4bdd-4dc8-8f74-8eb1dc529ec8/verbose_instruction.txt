1. Click the “Hardware” icon in the left-hand navigation bar and wait until the “Hardware Catalog” page is fully rendered (you should see a grid of product tiles).  
2. Click the first product tile labeled “Developer Laptop (Mac)” to open the product-detail page for the Mac developer laptop.  
3. Scroll to the section titled “Optional Software” and locate the three checkboxes: “Adobe Acrobat,” “Adobe Photoshop,” and “Eclipse IDE.”  
4. Click the checkbox in front of “Adobe Photoshop” so that a blue ✔ appears (this enables Photoshop).  
5. Verify that the checkboxes in front of “Adobe Acrobat” and “Eclipse IDE” are empty; if either one is checked, click it once to remove the checkmark.  
6. Scroll slightly to the text field labeled “Additional software requirements.” Click inside the field and type exactly:  
   Salesforce, QuickBooks, Zoom, Microsoft Office 365, Asana, Adobe Creative Cloud  
   (Note that we include all six items in a single comma-separated line.)  
7. Locate the “Quantity” dropdown on the right-hand side of the page. Click the dropdown to expand it, then click the value “3” from the list. Confirm the dropdown now shows “3.”  
8. Review the summary panel on the right: you should see “Developer Laptop (Mac), Qty: 3, Adobe Photoshop: Yes, Adobe Acrobat: No, Eclipse IDE: No, Additional software requirements: Salesforce...” If everything matches, continue.  
9. Click the green “Order Now” button at the bottom of the summary panel.  
10. Wait for the confirmation dialog to appear. Verify the confirmation message reads “Your order for 3 Developer Laptop (Mac) has been placed successfully” (or similar). Click “OK” to close the dialog.  
11. Open your email client (or the notification bell in the web UI) and check for an order confirmation message; confirm the quantity is 3 and the options match the selections we made.
