1. Click the “+” create-sheet icon located immediately to the left of the “Sheet1” tab; a new tab named “Sheet2” appears.  
2. Click cell A1 in “Sheet2”.  
3. Type Month and press Enter so the header is stored.  
4. Click cell B1.  
5. Type Total and press Enter to complete the second header.  
6. Switch to “Sheet1” by clicking its tab.  
7. Drag-select the range B1:G1 (these cells contain the abbreviated month names).  
8. Press Ctrl + C to copy the highlighted months; a moving dashed border should confirm the copy buffer is active.  
9. Switch back to “Sheet2”.  
10. Click cell A2 (the first body cell under the Month header).  
11. Right-click A2 and choose Paste special ➜ Transpose; the months now fill cells A2:A13 vertically.  
12. Switch again to “Sheet1”.  
13. Click cell B12 (directly beneath the January sales column).  
14. Type =SUM(B2:B11) and press Enter; cell B12 now displays January’s total.  
15. Hover over the small square at the bottom-right corner of B12 until the cursor becomes a thin black cross (the fill handle).  
16. Hold the mouse button down and drag horizontally to cell G12; release the button so Excel/Sheets auto-fills totals for February through June.  
17. Verify that cells B12:G12 now contain numeric totals (no #REF! errors).  
18. Drag-select B12:G12.  
19. Press Ctrl + X to cut the totals (the cells will show moving borders).  
20. Switch to “Sheet2”.  
21. Click cell B2 (the first body cell under the Total header).  
22. Right-click B2 and choose Paste special ➜ Transpose; the six totals are pasted vertically into B2:B13.  
23. Press Ctrl + S to save the workbook; confirm the filename and location if prompted.
