1. Open the Chromium browser icon on the left dock to switch to the running Airflow web interface.  
2. Locate the DAG list on the home screen and scroll until you see the row labelled “workFlow_Echo”.  
3. Click the grey Pause/Play slider just to the left of the DAG name so it turns green, un-pausing the DAG. (Airflow only allows manual triggers when the DAG is active.)  
4. Click directly on the blue “workFlow_Echo” DAG name to open its details page.  
5. In the top-center menu bar of the DAG details page, click the tab labeled “Graph” to switch into the Graph View.  
6. In Graph View, move your mouse to the upper-right corner and click the green “Play” (triangle) button to open the “Trigger DAG” dialog.  
7. Verify the “Run ID” auto-generated field looks correct (e.g., manual__<timestamp>), then click the blue “Trigger” button inside the dialog. Airflow immediately schedules the run.  
8. Wait a few seconds; the mini status badge in the top-left of Graph View changes from “no status” to “queued” and then to “running”.  
9. Watch the task boxes in the graph: they will turn from light-grey to yellow (queued) and finally to green (success) or red (failed). This provides real-time confirmation that the DAG was triggered from Graph View.  
10. When every task box is green, the run has completed successfully. You can also confirm by looking at the “Runs” dropdown in the upper-right; it should show the latest run with a green checkmark.
