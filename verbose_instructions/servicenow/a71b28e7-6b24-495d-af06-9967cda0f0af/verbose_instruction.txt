1. Click the “Hardware” panel in the main navigation bar and wait until the catalog grid finishes loading (watch for the spinning loader to disappear).  
2. Click inside the top-right search box labeled “Search catalog”.  
3. Type the exact phrase Apple MacBook Pro 15" (include the double quote) and press Enter to apply the search filter.  
4. Verify the results list now shows a single card titled “Apple MacBook Pro 15"” with an Apple logo thumbnail.  
5. Click the product card to open the product-detail pane on the right side of the screen.  
6. Click the Quantity dropdown inside the product-detail pane; choose the option 5 from the numeric list.  
7. Confirm the Total updates to reflect 5 units (price × 5) just below the dropdown.  
8. Click the blue Order Now button located at the bottom of the pane.  
9. Wait for the success toast that reads “Request placed successfully” and note the order reference number displayed in the toast for future tracking.  
10. Switch to the Orders tab in the main navigation bar, locate today’s entry with the same reference number, and ensure the status column shows “Pending — Supplier” to confirm the order has been logged in the system.
