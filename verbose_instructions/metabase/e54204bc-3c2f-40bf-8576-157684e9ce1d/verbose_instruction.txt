1. Click the New button in the top-right corner of the Metabase toolbar  
2. Select Question from the drop-down panel that appears  
3. Choose Raw Data > Sample Database in the data picker on the left side  
4. Click the Orders table in the list of tables so it turns blue  
5. Click the blue Visualize button in the bottom-right corner of the screen  
6. Click the Visualization (bar-chart) icon in the bottom-left to open the Visualization sidebar  
7. Click the Line option in the sidebar so the preview switches to a blank line chart  
8. Click the Summarize button (∑ icon) in the top-right of the editor panel  
9. Click the small x next to Count in the Summarize by box to remove it  
10. Click + Add metric, scroll, and choose Sum of Total so “Sum of Total” appears under Summarize by  
11. Click + Add grouping, expand Created At, and choose by month so “Created At – Month” shows under Group by  
12. Click Done at the bottom of the Summarize modal to apply the aggregation  
13. Observe that the canvas now shows a multi-point line labeled Sum of Total by Created At – Month (confirm values look reasonable)  
14. Click the Visualization (bar-chart) icon again to reopen the sidebar if it closed  
15. Click Line in the sidebar to reveal Line settings tabs  
16. Click the Display tab underneath the Line settings heading  
17. Toggle Goal line on (slider turns blue)  
18. Type 40000 in the Goal value input box and press Enter; verify a horizontal dotted line appears near 40 k on the y-axis  
19. Toggle Trend line on (slider turns blue) and confirm a faint sloped line appears across the points  
20. Click Done at the bottom of the sidebar to save the display options  
21. Hover over several points on the chart to verify tooltips show “Sum of Total” values—this confirms grouping and metric are correct  
22. Click the cloud-shaped Download full results button in the bottom-right corner of the question editor  
23. Select .png from the download format list; Metabase generates the file and the browser download toast appears  
24. Switch to the file manager (Files app) by clicking its folder icon in the dock  
25. Navigate to /home/user/Downloads in the left sidebar; you should see a file named something like Orders-line-chart.png  
26. Right-click that file and choose Rename  
27. Type line_chart.png and press Enter to confirm the new name  
28. Press Ctrl + C (or right-click > Copy) on line_chart.png to copy the file  
29. Click Desktop in the left sidebar, then press Ctrl + V (or right-click > Paste) to place the file on the Desktop  
30. Double-click line_chart.png on the Desktop to open it and visually verify it shows the goal line at 40 k and a slanted trend line—now the task is complete
