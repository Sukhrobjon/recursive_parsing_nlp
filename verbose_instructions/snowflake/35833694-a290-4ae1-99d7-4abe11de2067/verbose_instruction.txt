1. Click the left-hand navigation item “Data” and then click the sub-item “Databases”.  
2. Click the blue “+ Database” button on the upper-right of the Databases page.  
3. Type WIND_DB in the “Name” field of the modal dialog and press the “Create” button.  
4. Locate the new WIND_DB entry in the center pane; click “WIND_DB ▸ PUBLIC” to select the default PUBLIC schema (if it is not visible, click the 3-dot icon above the list and choose “Refresh” first).  
5. Click the “Create” button in the right-hand details panel, hover over “Table”, and choose “From File”.  
6. In the file-loader dialog, click “Browse”, navigate to Desktop, select wind_dataset.csv, and choose “Open”.  
7. Scroll to the “Name” field in the same dialog, type WIND as the target table name, then click the “Load” button at the bottom-right.  
8. Wait for the green “Successfully Loaded Data” message, then click “Done”.

9. Click “Projects” in the left navigation and then “Worksheets”.  
10. Click the “+” icon at the top of the worksheets list and choose “SQL Worksheet”.  
11. At the top of the new worksheet, click the grey “No database selected” selector, pick Database: WIND_DB and Schema: PUBLIC, then click outside the selector to close it.  
12. Copy and paste the following query into the worksheet editor:

```sql
SELECT
        SUBSTR(date, 1, 4)        AS YEAR,
        AVG(wind)                 AS AVG_WIND_SPEED
FROM    WIND
GROUP BY
        YEAR
ORDER BY
        YEAR;
```

13. Click the blue “Run” triangle in the upper-right of the worksheet; confirm the result grid now shows two columns: YEAR and AVG_WIND_SPEED.

14. In the worksheet list on the left, hover over your current worksheet name, click the 3-dot icon, choose “Move → + New Dashboard”.  
15. In the “Dashboard name” field type wind_dashboard, then click “Create Dashboard”.

16. The worksheet is now embedded as a tile in wind_dashboard. Click the 3-dot icon on the tile’s upper-right corner and choose “View Table” so the full result grid is visible.  
17. Click “Download results” on the result grid’s header and choose “Download as .csv”. A file named similar to query_result_<timestamp>.csv is saved to the browser’s default Downloads folder.  
18. Click the “Chart” tab located just above the result grid.  
19. In the chart controls on the right, set “Chart type” to “Bar”. Verify the X-axis shows YEAR and the Y-axis shows AVG_WIND_SPEED.  
20. Click “Download chart” (down-arrow icon) in the upper-right of the chart area; the file downloads as a PNG image.

21. Switch to the terminal (or file explorer in the virtual machine).  
22. Run the following two commands to rename the downloaded artifacts:

```bash
mv ~/Downloads/query_result_*.csv ~/Downloads/avg_wind_speed.csv
mv ~/Downloads/chart_*.png        ~/Downloads/avg_speed_chart.png
```
(Adjust paths if your environment stores downloads elsewhere.)

23. Return to the Chromium browser tab titled “wind_dashboard” by clicking its tab in the top bar; you are now back on the dashboard page and ready for further work.
