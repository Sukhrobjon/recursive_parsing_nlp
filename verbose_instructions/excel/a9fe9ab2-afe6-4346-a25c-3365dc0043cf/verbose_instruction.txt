1. Click cell J2 in Sheet1.  
2. Type `=B2-C2-D2-F2-G2-H2` into the Formula Bar and press ENTER to calculate the Gross Profit for the first record.  
3. Click cell J2 again and move the cursor to the small square “fill handle” at the bottom-right corner of the cell.  
4. Drag the fill handle straight down until you reach the last used row in column J (for example, row 10). Release the mouse button so the Gross Profit formula replicates for every row in the column.  
5. Quickly verify a few random cells (e.g., J5 and J9) by selecting them and checking that their formula displays correctly in the Formula Bar.  
6. Click the plus (+) icon labeled “New sheet” to the right of the Sheet1 tab to create a new worksheet.  
7. Immediately type `Sheet2` and press ENTER to rename the new worksheet.  
8. Switch to Sheet2 by clicking its tab.  
9. Click cell A1.  
10. Type `Year_Profit` and press ENTER to establish the column header.  
11. Click cell A2.  
12. Type `=CONCATENATE(Sheet1!A2,"_",Sheet1!J2)` and press ENTER to join the Year from Sheet1 column A with the calculated Gross Profit from Sheet1 column J, separated by an underscore.  
13. Click cell A2 again, grab the fill handle at the bottom-right corner, and drag it down through the last matching row (e.g., A10). Release to copy the concatenation formula for every record.  
14. Inspect two or three cells in column A (Sheet2) to confirm that each displays text in the pattern `Year_GrossProfit` (for instance, `2021_15000`).  
15. Press Ctrl+S to save the workbook and preserve all changes.
