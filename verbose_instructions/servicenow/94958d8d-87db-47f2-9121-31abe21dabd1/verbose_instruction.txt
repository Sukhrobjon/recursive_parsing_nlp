1. Click the funnel-shaped Filter icon located in the center of the top menu bar (directly beneath the page title “servicenow”); wait until the “Order results by the following fields” panel appears.  
2. Click the blue “Add Sort” button that sits on the first row of the panel; a new sort row with two dropdowns (“Field” and “Direction”) will be inserted.  
3. Click the first dropdown in this new row, start typing “Enable Multifactor Authentication” in the search box that pops up, then press Enter to select that exact field.  
4. Click the adjacent “Direction” dropdown (it shows “a to z” by default) and choose “z to a” so the field will sort in descending order.  
5. Click the “Add Sort” button a second time to insert another sort row underneath the first one.  
6. Click the “Field” dropdown of this second row, type “Prefix”, and press Enter to pick the “Prefix” field.  
7. Click the “Direction” dropdown next to it and again choose “z to a” to set descending order for Prefix.  
8. Click the green “Run” button in the lower-right corner of the panel to execute the sort; the user list will refresh.  
9. Verify that the table now shows users grouped by “Enable Multifactor Authentication” status (those with MFA enabled on top) and, within each MFA status group, ordered by “Prefix” from Z down to A.
