1. Open a browser tab and Navigate to console.cloud.google.com/bigquery so the BigQuery web UI loads.  
2. Wait until the left-hand Explorer pane appears, then Click the blue “+ Add” button to the right of the word “Explorer”.  
3. In the “Add data” dialog that pops up, Click the radio-button row labeled “Public datasets” (the icon looks like a small storefront).  
4. In the new Marketplace panel, Click once inside the search bar beneath the “Marketplace” heading, Type baseball, and Press Enter.  
5. Locate the single search result card titled “MLB 2016 Pitch-by-Pitch” (the publisher shows “bigquery-public-data”). Click the dataset name to open its details page.  
6. After the details load, Click the blue “VIEW DATASET” button in the top-right corner of the card. A new browser tab opens that shows the dataset schema and tables.  
7. Switch to the newly opened tab by Clicking on it; we can see the dataset identifier bigquery-public-data.baseball in the header area along with a list of tables such as games_wide, pitch_by_pitch, and person_codes.  
8. Return to the original BigQuery tab, Observe that the Explorer pane now contains a new entry bigquery-public-data ➞ baseball. Click the small ► triangle next to baseball to expand it and verify that the same tables are listed.  
9. Click the table pitch_by_pitch and then Click “Preview” in the right-hand panel; verify that a sample of rows (pitch_type, batter_id, pitcher_id, etc.) appears, confirming the dataset is accessible.  
10. (Optional quick test) Click “Query” then paste the SQL below into the editor to count rows, and Click the blue “Run” button:  
```sql
SELECT COUNT(1) AS total_pitches
FROM `bigquery-public-data.baseball.pitch_by_pitch`;
```  
11. Confirm the Job completes with a green check-mark and the Results grid displays a non-zero total_pitches value, proving the dataset was added successfully.
