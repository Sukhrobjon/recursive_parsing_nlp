1. Click inside the empty input box next to the label “Problem statement” in the central column.  
2. Type Issue in connecting to internet using modem. and press Tab to move focus away so the value is recorded.  
3. Locate the drop-down list labeled “Impact” in the right-hand column (it is the first selector in that column).  
4. Verify the displayed value shows 3 - Low; if it shows anything else, click the list, scroll (if needed), and click 3 - Low to select it.  
5. Find the drop-down list labeled “Urgency” directly below “Priority” (third selector in the right column).  
6. Confirm the value is 3 - Low; if it is not, click the list and choose 3 - Low from the menu.  
7. Leave the “Service offering” and “Assignment group” fields unchanged—both should remain blank.  
8. Move your cursor to the upper-right corner of the form and click the blue Submit button.  
9. Wait until the page refreshes back to the problem list; absence of an error banner indicates the record was saved successfully.
