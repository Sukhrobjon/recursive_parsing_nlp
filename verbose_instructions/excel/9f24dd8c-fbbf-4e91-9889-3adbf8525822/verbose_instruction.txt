1. Click the funnel-shaped AutoFilter icon on the main toolbar to activate filtering on the header row.  
2. Click the small triangle at the right edge of cell E1 that contains the text “Sales Channel”.  
3. Uncheck the box labeled “(Select All)” so that every channel is deselected.  
4. Check the box labeled “E-mail Coupon” to keep only those rows visible.  
5. Click the “OK” button in the filter popup; the sheet now shows only the header row and the rows where “Sales Channel” equals “E-mail Coupon”.  
6. Click the “+” (Append sheet) icon just to the left of the “Sheet1” tab at the bottom; a new tab named “Sheet2” appears.  
7. Click the “Sheet1” tab to return to the filtered data.  
8. Click the column header “A” to highlight the entire Date column.  
9. Press Ctrl + C to copy that column.  
10. Click the “Sheet2” tab at the bottom to switch sheets.  
11. Click cell A1 so it is highlighted.  
12. Press Ctrl + V to paste the Date values into column A of Sheet2.  
13. Click the “Sheet1” tab again to go back.  
14. Click the column header “G” to highlight the entire Revenue ($) column (confirm that the header actually shows “Revenue ($)”).  
15. Press Ctrl + C to copy the Revenue ($) column.  
16. Click the “Sheet2” tab to return.  
17. Click cell B1 in Sheet2 so it is selected.  
18. Press Ctrl + V to paste the Revenue ($) values into column B.  
19. Press Ctrl + S (or Cmd + S on macOS) to save the workbook and preserve the new “Sheet2” with the filtered Date and Revenue ($) data.
