1. Click the grey gear icon labeled “Settings” in the upper-right corner of the Metabase page  
2. Choose “Admin settings” from the drop-down menu that appears  
3. Click the “Databases” tab in the blue navigation bar at the top of the Admin panel  
4. Locate and click the blue link “Bring the Sample Database back” that appears in the empty-state banner  
5. Wait until the green toast notification “Sample Database restored” appears in the lower-left corner – this confirms the database is re-created  
6. Scroll through the list on the Databases page and verify that “Sample Database” now shows status “✅ Connected” with the description “Metabase internal demo dataset”
