1. Click the Chromium icon (or your default browser) on the left dock to bring the Airflow web-UI to the foreground.  
2. Locate the DAGs list in the Home screen and type workFlow_Echo into the Search DAGs box to filter the table.  
3. Click the blue text workFlow_Echo in the DAG column to open the DAG Detail page.  
4. Look at the Runs bar chart (histogram) near the top-left; verify that the first bar (furthest to the right) is labeled “most recent run” when you hover over it.  
5. Find the row labeled processing directly beneath the histogram.  
6. Click the right-most square in that processing row (this square corresponds to the most recent run because it is vertically aligned with the right-most bar you just identified).  
7. Wait for the Task Instance page to load, then click the Logs tab in the right-hand details panel (it may be already selected; if so, it will be highlighted in blue).  
8. Observe that a streaming log panel appears with a green “Task exited with return code 0” (or any other status); this confirms you are viewing the logs for task_id processing from the latest workFlow_Echo run.
