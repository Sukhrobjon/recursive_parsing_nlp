1) Open the Airbyte UI in your browser and wait until the Connections list loads  
2) Click the row named “Sample Data (Faker) -> Local CSV” to open its Connection Overview page  
3) Click the “Replication” tab located between “Status” and “Job History” so the replication settings appear  
4) Click the “Configuration” accordion to expand it; we now see two editable fields: “Schedule type” and “Replication frequency”  
5) Click the dropdown to the right of “Schedule type”, then select “Cron” (this unlocks the custom cron expression box)  
6) Click into the cron expression text box that is now visible under “Replication frequency”, delete any existing value, and type the exact string  
   ```
   0 0 18 * * ?
   ```  
7) Observe the helper text directly below the box—Airbyte should display “At 06:00 PM” confirming the cron was parsed correctly  
8) Scroll to the bottom-right corner of the page and click the blue “Save changes” button  
9) Wait for the green toast message “Connection saved successfully” to appear—this verifies the new daily 18:00 schedule is active
