1. Click the “Hardware” menu item in the left navigation bar; the page refreshes and displays the hardware product catalog.  
2. Click inside the top-right search field labeled “Search catalog”.  
3. Type Apple Watch and press Enter to execute the search; the items list now shows only Apple Watch variants.  
4. Click the product card titled “Apple Watch” (the card with the Apple logo thumbnail and price tag).  
5. Wait until the product-detail pane appears on the right side of the screen; verify the title reads “Apple Watch” to confirm you opened the correct item.  
6. Click the drop-down box labeled “Quantity” directly beneath the price.  
7. Select 5 from the quantity list (or type 5 if the field is editable) so the total quantity shows “5”.  
8. Click the green “Order Now” button at the bottom of the pane to submit the order.  
9. Wait for the confirmation toast that says “Order placed successfully” and note the order number displayed.  
10. Navigate to the “Orders” tab in the top navigation bar; verify a new entry for “Apple Watch” with Quantity = 5 and Status = “Processing” appears, confirming the order was recorded.
