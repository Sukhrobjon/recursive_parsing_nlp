1. Click “Charts” in the top-level navigation bar so we land on the chart gallery.  
2. Click “+ CHART” in the upper-right corner to open the Create Chart modal.  
3. Click the blue “Choose a dataset” dropdown, scroll (or type in the filter) and select the dataset “flights”.  
4. In the chart-type panel on the right, click “Line Chart” (the icon showing connected points).  
5. Click the blue “CREATE NEW CHART” button at the bottom of the modal; we are now taken to the chart builder.  
6. Drag the field “Travel Date” from the left sidebar into the “X-AXIS” well in the center pane.  
7. Drag the field “Cost” from the sidebar into the “METRICS” well.  
8. Click the small pill that now shows “Cost (SUM)” inside the METRICS well to open its configuration.  
9. Click the “AGGREGATE” dropdown and verify “SUM” is selected (choose it if it is not); press “SAVE” in that pop-up so the metric reads “SUM(Cost)”.  
10. Scroll down on the main configuration panel until the grey accordion labeled “Advanced analytics” is visible.  
11. Click the “Advanced analytics” accordion to expand it.  
12. Click the “RULE” dropdown; in the calendar frequencies list choose “7 calendar day frequency”.  
13. Click the “FILL METHOD” dropdown just beneath RULE and choose “Median value” so the system will compute the weekly median of our 7-day windows.  
14. Scroll back to the top of the right panel and click the blue “CREATE CHART” button; the preview canvas now refreshes.  
15. Wait a few seconds until the line plot appears—first we check that the x-axis shows weekly ticks (e.g., “2024-05-05”, “2024-05-12”, …) and the tooltip for any point shows “SUM(Cost) – median over 7 days” (confirming the resampling worked).  
16. Click the green “SAVE” button in the upper-right corner of the screen.  
17. Type resampling in the “CHART NAME” text box.  
18. Confirm the “Save to” folder is correct (leave as default or choose the folder mandated by your workspace).  
19. Click the blue “SAVE” button—Superset/Metabase now stores the chart.  
20. Observe the toast notification “Chart saved” and verify that the title bar at the top of the chart reads “resampling”; we can now see it listed under Charts → Recently Saved.
