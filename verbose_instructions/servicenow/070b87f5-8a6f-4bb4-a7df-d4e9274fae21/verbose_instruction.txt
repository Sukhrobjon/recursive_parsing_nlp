1. Open your web browser and go to the hardware store’s website (e.g., type the URL in the address bar and press Enter).  
2. Click the magnifying-glass icon (or the “Search” field) in the top-right corner of the homepage.  
3. Type “iPad mini” in the search box and press Enter to bring up product results.  
4. Locate the product tile that exactly matches “iPad mini” (verify the latest generation if multiple appear). Click the product name or image to open its product detail page.  
5. Inside the product page, click the drop-down labeled “Choose the colour.” Select “Purple” from the list. (The product photo should update to a purple device—confirm visually before proceeding.)  
6. Click the second drop-down labeled “Choose the storage.” Select “256 GB.”  
7. Click the quantity field (typically set to “1” by default). Type “8” or use the up-arrow until the quantity reads 8.  
8. Click the “Add to Cart” or “Add to Basket” button (often a solid-colored button near the price). Wait for the confirmation pop-up or mini-cart indicator to update to “8” items.  
9. Click the shopping-cart icon in the site’s header (usually top-right). This opens the cart summary page.  
10. Review the cart line item: confirm “iPad mini,” Colour = Purple, Storage = 256 GB, Quantity = 8, and verify the unit price and subtotal.  
11. Click the “Checkout” or “Proceed to Checkout” button (often highlighted) beneath the order summary.  
12. Sign in with your store account if prompted; otherwise, choose “Guest Checkout” and enter your email so you receive order confirmation later.  
13. Fill in shipping information: name, address, phone number. Double-check the address for accuracy—it will appear on the delivery label.  
14. Click “Continue” or “Next” to reach the shipping-method section. Select your preferred shipping speed (e.g., “Standard” or “Express”).  
15. Click “Continue” to advance to payment. Enter payment details (credit card, debit card, PayPal, or store account).  
16. Review the final order summary one last time—ensure it still reads 8 × iPad mini (Purple, 256 GB).  
17. Click the “Place Order,” “Submit Order,” or similarly labeled button to finalize the purchase.  
18. Wait for the confirmation screen; note the order number and estimated delivery date. We will use this reference if any follow-up is required.  
19. Open your email inbox and verify the hardware store sent a confirmation message—this serves as proof the order for eight purple 256 GB iPad minis is successfully placed.
