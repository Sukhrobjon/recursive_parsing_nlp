1. Open your web browser and navigate to https://console.cloud.google.com/bigquery so we are on the BigQuery console.  
2. Click the blue “SELECT PROJECT” button in the blue banner (if it appears) and choose the project that currently contains the obsolete dataset; then click “OPEN”.  
3. Locate the left-hand “Explorer” panel and Click the small grey disclosure triangle “►” (it becomes “▼” when open) next to the project name to expand all resources.  
4. Scroll inside the Explorer list and find the dataset label customer_orders.  
5. Hover over customer_orders and Click the vertical-ellipsis icon “⋮” that appears to the right of the dataset name.  
6. Click the menu item “Delete” in the drop-down that opens.  
7. Wait for the “Delete dataset” dialog to appear, read the warning text, then Click inside the confirmation textbox labeled “Type delete to confirm”.  
8. Type exactly delete (lower-case, no quotes) in that textbox to acknowledge the permanent removal.  
9. Click the red “DELETE” button in the lower-right corner of the dialog.  
10. Watch the status toast in the lower-left corner of the console; it should display “Dataset ‘customer_orders’ deleted.” within a few seconds.  
11. Verify deletion by looking back at the Explorer panel—ensure customer_orders no longer appears under the project list. If it still shows, Click the circular arrow “Refresh” icon at the top of the Explorer panel and confirm it is gone.  
12. Optionally, open the “Activity” tab (left sidebar → Activity) and check the newest log entry; we can see an entry “Delete Dataset” with status “OK” confirming the operation completed successfully.
