1) Open your web browser and navigate to the Metabase URL shown on-screen (typically http://localhost:3000 if you just started the Docker container).  
2) Wait until the “Welcome to Metabase” page renders, then click the blue button “Let’s get started”.  
3) Choose language by clicking the drop-down, select “English”, and click “Next” in the lower-right corner.  
4) Fill the “User profile” form exactly as listed in setup.json:  
   • Click the “First name” field and type `John`  
   • Click the “Last name” field and type `Wilson`  
   • Click the “Your email” field and type `johnwilson@gmail.com`  
   • Click the “Company or team name” field and type `Google`  
   • Click the “Create a password” field and type `Spider2.0`  
   • Click the “Confirm your password” field and type `Spider2.0` again (Metabase will show a green check mark when the two passwords match).  
5) Click the “Next” button to move on.  
6) When asked “How are you planning to use Metabase?”, click any option (e.g., the middle tile “Not sure yet”), then click “Next”.  
7) In the “Add your data” screen, click the “PostgreSQL” card (it has the elephant logo).  
8) Complete the PostgreSQL connection form using the values from setup.json:  
   • Click “Display name” and type `PostgresData`  
   • Click “Host” and type `localhost`  
   • Click “Port” and type `5432` (verify no spaces)  
   • Click “Database name” and type `metabase`  
   • Click “Database username” and type `user`  
   • Click “Database password” and type `password`  
   (Metabase will automatically try to validate the port number; make sure the red error banner disappears before continuing.)  
9) Scroll to the bottom of the page (or press the Page-Down key) and click the blue “Connect database” button.  
10) Wait until you see a green success toast “Successfully connected!” at the lower-left corner—this confirms the credentials are correct.  
11) In the “Help improve Metabase?” screen, switch the toggle off (left) so it shows grey, meaning “Disable usage data collection”, in line with step 9 of the plan.  
12) Click the “Finish” button.  
13) On the final splash page, click the link “Take me to Metabase” to reach the main dashboard.  
14) Verify the setup:  
   • Observe your name “John Wilson” in the top-right user menu.  
   • Hover over the navigation sidebar and confirm “PostgresData” appears under the “Browse data” section.  
   • Click “PostgresData” once; you should see schema names from the `metabase` database, proving the connection works.  
15) (Optional) Log out by clicking the avatar → “Sign out”, then log back in with `johnwilson@gmail.com` / `Spider2.0` to ensure the credentials were stored successfully.
