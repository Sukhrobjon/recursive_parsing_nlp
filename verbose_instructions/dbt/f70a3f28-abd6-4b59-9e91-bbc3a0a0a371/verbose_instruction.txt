1. Click the green button labeled “Create Environment” at the bottom of the page.  
2. Click inside the “Environment name” text field that appears in the modal.  
3. Type Continuous_Integration to replace the default value.  
4. Click the card or radio button labeled “Staging” in the “Set deployment type” section so that it turns blue (selected).  
5. Click the checkbox next to “Only run on a custom branch”; notice a new input called “custom branch” now appears.  
6. Click inside the newly visible “custom branch” text field.  
7. Type main in that text field to set the branch restriction.  
8. Click the blue “Save” button in the upper-right corner of the modal to persist the new staging environment.  
9. Verify the new environment appears in your environment list with:  
   • Name: Continuous_Integration  
   • Type: Staging  
   • Branch restriction: main
