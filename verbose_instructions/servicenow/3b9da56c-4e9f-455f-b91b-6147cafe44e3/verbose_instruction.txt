1. Open your web browser and navigate to the hardware store’s site (e.g., https://hardware.example.com).  
2. Click the “Sign In” button in the upper-right corner and log in with your corporate SSO credentials (email + password or “Login with Google/Microsoft” as required).  
3. Click in the top-center Search bar, type “Loaner Laptop”, and press Enter.  
4. Scan the search results list and click the product card titled “Loaner Laptop” (make sure the SKU or part number matches your company’s standard if multiple appear).  
5. On the product detail page, click the Quantity field, type 9, and press Enter so the total quantity shows “9”.  
6. Scroll to the “Configuration” or “Customize” section directly beneath the price.  
7. Click the dropdown labeled “How long do you need it for ?”, then select “1 day” from the list.  
8. Click the second dropdown labeled “When do you need it ?”, then choose the option “By the end of the month”.  
9. Verify both configuration selections now display:  
   • How long do you need it for ?  1 day  
   • When do you need it ?          By the end of the month  
10. Click the blue “Add to Cart” button on the right-hand panel; wait until the confirmation banner (“Added to cart”) appears at the top.  
11. Click the Cart icon (shopping-cart graphic) in the upper-right corner to open the Cart drawer.  
12. Confirm the line item shows “Loaner Laptop × 9” and the two configuration notes under it; adjust if needed by pressing the pencil icon and repeating steps 5–8.  
13. Click the “Checkout” button at the bottom of the Cart drawer.  
14. On the Shipping page, verify your default address; if necessary, click “Change Address” and select the correct office location.  
15. Click “Continue to Payment”; if a purchase order (PO) number is required, type it into the “PO Number” text field.  
16. Review the Order Summary on the right: quantity 9, configurations listed, tax and shipping estimates.  
17. Click the green “Place Order” button. Wait for the order confirmation page to load.  
18. Note the Order Number displayed in bold (e.g., “Order #HW-35467”)—copy it to Slack or email for team tracking.
