1. Locate the ServiceNow browser tab that is already open.  
2. Click the “All” icon (three-line hamburger menu) at the upper-left corner of the navigation bar to open the application navigator.  
3. Click once inside the search field labeled “Filter navigator”.  
4. Type the word `Leases` and wait for the filtered results to appear beneath the search box.  
5. Observe that a single matching line appears: “Leases” indented under the parent “Contracts” application group.  
6. Click the “Leases” link (make sure it is the child under “Contracts”, not any similarly named global search item).  
7. Wait for the main content frame to reload; confirm you see a list view whose title bar reads “Leases” and whose breadcrumb shows “Contracts > Leases”.
