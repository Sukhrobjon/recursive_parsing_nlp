1) Open Chrome (or any browser you prefer)  
2) Navigate to lookerstudio.google.com and sign in with the Google account that owns the report  
3) Click the “Reports” icon (the stacked-square icon on the left sidebar)  
4) Type Statistics in the search bar at the top of the Reports list and press Enter  
5) Click the Statistics report name to open it in Edit mode  
6) Click the blue Share button in the upper-right corner and choose Embed report… from the dropdown  
7) Disable three visual elements in the Embed settings panel that appears on the right:  
   • Toggle off Show report navigation (this hides the top navigation)  
   • Toggle off Show report title (this hides the title)  
   • Toggle off Include filter bar (this removes the filter bar)  
8) Click the blue COPY button under “Embed URL” (we’ll use the link in the next step)  
9) Open a new browser tab, paste the copied URL into the address bar, and press Enter; the Statistics dashboard now renders with no top navigation, no title, and no filter bar—exactly as requested
