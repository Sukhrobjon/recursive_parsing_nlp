1. Click the gear icon ⚙️ located in the upper-right corner of Metabase.  
2. Select “Admin settings” from the drop-down menu that appears.  
3. Click the “Databases” tab (it sits in the top navigation bar of the Admin panel).  
4. Locate the row labeled “Sample Database” in the list; click directly on the blue text “Sample Database” to open its settings page.  
5. Click the red link “Remove this database” on the right side of the settings page.  
6. Type the exact text Sample Database into the confirmation field that pops up (Metabase is case-sensitive here, so make sure the capitalization matches).  
7. Click the red “Delete” button at the bottom of the confirmation dialog.  
8. Wait until the dialog closes and the Databases list refreshes—“Sample Database” should no longer appear in the table.  
9. Press Ctrl + R (or ⌘ + R on macOS) to refresh the browser page once more; confirm that “Sample Database” is still absent, ensuring the deletion persisted.  
10. Navigate back to the main Metabase home screen by clicking the Metabase logo in the upper-left corner; we can see the sidebar’s “Browse data” section is now cleared of the Sample Database, verifying the panel is clean.
