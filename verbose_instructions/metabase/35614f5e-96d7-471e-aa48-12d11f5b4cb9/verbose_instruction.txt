1. Click the “New” button in the upper-right corner of Metabase.  
2. Choose “Question” from the drop-down panel that appears.  
3. Click “Sample Database” in the “Pick your data” modal.  
4. Select the “Orders” table, then click the blue “Next” (or “→”) button at the bottom.  

5. Click “Join data” (link-icon) in the top gray bar of the query builder.  
6. In the “Pick a table” dialog, choose “Products”.  
7. Under “How do these tables relate?”, set:  
   • “Orders → Product ID” equals “Products → ID”.  
8. Leave the join type as “Inner join” (default) and click “Done”.  
   Note that an inner join limits results to rows where the Product IDs match; that is exactly what we need.  

9. Click the blue “Summarize” button (top-right of the builder).  
10. In the pop-up, under “Pick the metric you want to see”, click “Add” > choose “Count of rows”.  
11. Under “Group by”, click “Add” > choose “Products → Category” (labeled “Product Category”).  
12. Click “Done” to close the Summarize box.  

13. Click the “Sort” button (two-arrow icon) in the top bar.  
14. In the sort modal, choose:  
    • Column: “Count of rows”  
    • Order: “Descending”  
15. Click “Done”. We can already see the rows are sorted from most to least counts—good verification.  

16. Click the “Visualization” button (paintbrush icon) at the bottom to open the Visualization side panel.  
17. In the panel’s dropdown, choose “Funnel” as the visualization type.  
18. Observe the preview updates to a funnel chart; confirm the categories appear from top (highest count) to bottom (lowest).  
19. Click the blue “Done” button in the Visualization panel to save the chart settings.  

20. Click the cloud-shaped “Download” button in the lower-right corner of the screen.  
21. In the “Download” dialog, select the “.png image” option.  
22. Click “Download” and wait for the file to finish saving to your browser’s default download folder (usually “~/Downloads”).  

23. Open your file browser (Switch to “File Explorer/Finder”).  
24. Navigate to the Downloads folder; you should see a PNG with a timestamped name (e.g., “Metabase-2024-...png”).  
25. Right-click the file, choose “Rename”, and type exactly:  
    funnel_chart.png  
26. Press Enter/Return to confirm the new name.  

27. (Optional verification) Double-click “funnel_chart.png” to open it—confirm the funnel reflects Product Category counts sorted descendingly.  

You now have the completed funnel chart stored as “funnel_chart.png”.
