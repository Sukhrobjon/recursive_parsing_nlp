1. Confirm the header row: verify that cell A1 reads “Week”, B1 reads “Sales”, and C1 reads “COGS”. We will use these three columns to build the summary sentence for every record.  
2. Click cell D1 (the first blank column to the right of “COGS”).  
3. Type “Week Sales COGS Summary” and press ENTER. This labels the new helper column.  
4. Click cell D2 (first data row of the new column).  
5. Type the following formula exactly, including quotation marks and commas, then press ENTER:  
   ```excel
   =CONCAT("Week: ", A2, ", Sales: ", B2, ", COGS: ", C2)
   ```  
   Note that:  
   • CONCAT joins text fragments in order.  
   • A2, B2, C2 pull the current row’s Week, Sales, and COGS values.  
6. Hover over the small square “fill handle” in the bottom-right corner of cell D2 until the cursor turns into a thin black cross.  
7. Double-click the fill handle. Excel auto-copies the formula down to the last contiguous row of data in columns A–C, generating a sentence for every record.  
8. Scroll to the last populated row in column D and spot-check a few entries to ensure the pattern reads like:  
   Week: 12, Sales: 34 000, COGS: 21 500  
   (Your numbers will match each row’s underlying data.)  
9. Press Ctrl+S to save the workbook so the newly added “Week Sales COGS Summary” column and formulas persist.
