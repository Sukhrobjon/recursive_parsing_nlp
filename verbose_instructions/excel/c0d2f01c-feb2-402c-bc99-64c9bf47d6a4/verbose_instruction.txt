1. Click cell B2 to activate the cell that already contains the acceleration formula.  
2. Drag the small square (fill handle) in the bottom-right corner of B2 straight down to the last data row you need (for example B30) so Excel auto-copies the formula through the entire B column range.  
3. Click cell D2 to select the first cell of the other calculated column.  
4. Drag the fill handle of D2 straight down to the matching last row (for example D30) so every row in column D now holds the same formula pattern.  
5. Click cell E1—the next empty column header—to prepare the new output column.  
6. Type the text Combined Data and press Enter so the header is stored in E1.  
7. Click cell E2 (first data row under the new header) to begin the concatenation formula.  
8. Type or paste the formula below exactly, which builds the “Header: value” pattern with two-decimal rounding:  

```excel
=$A$1 & ": " & FIXED(A2, 2) & ", " &
 $B$1 & ": " & FIXED(B2, 2) & ", " &
 $C$1 & ": " & FIXED(C2, 2) & ", " &
 $D$1 & ": " & FIXED(D2, 2)
```  

9. Press Enter; E2 now displays the combined, rounded text for row 2.  
10. Drag the fill handle of E2 down to the same last row used in steps 2 and 4 (for example E30) so every row receives its own concatenated string.  
11. Scroll through column E to verify each entry follows the pattern “Header: 00.00, … , Header: 00.00” and that the numeric parts show exactly two decimals.  
12. Press Ctrl+S to save all completed calculations and the new Combined Data column.
