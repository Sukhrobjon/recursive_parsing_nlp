1. Click the top-level menu item “Charts” so we land on the main Charts gallery.  
2. Click the blue “+ CHART” button in the upper-right corner to start a new visualization.  
3. Click the “Choose a dataset” drop-down that pops up in the modal.  
4. Select the dataset named flights (you should now see “flights” highlighted).  
5. In the right-hand pane, click the thumbnail labeled “Line Chart” under the Time Series section.  
6. Click the blue “CREATE NEW CHART” button at the bottom of the modal; the Chart Builder opens.  
7. Drag the field “Travel Date” from the left Data panel into the X-AXIS input box—this tells the chart to plot by date.  
8. Drag the field “Cost” from the same Data panel into the METRICS box.  
9. Click the small caret ▼ inside the METRICS pill to expand its settings.  
10. Click the “Aggregate” selector and choose “AVG” so the metric now reads AVG(Cost).  
11. Click the green “SAVE” button inside that metrics pop-over to confirm.  
12. Click the large blue “CREATE CHART” button at the bottom of the screen to render the initial line chart.  
13. Scroll down the right configuration sidebar until you see the accordion titled “Advanced analytics.”  
14. Click “Advanced analytics” to expand it.  
15. Click the toggle or > icon next to “Rolling function” so a new set of controls appears.  
16. Select “mean” from the “Rolling function” drop-down (we want a rolling mean, not sum or std).  
17. Type 7 in the “Periods” input—this defines our 7-day window.  
18. Type 7 in the “Min periods” input so partial windows are not included.  
19. Click the blue “CREATE CHART” button again; the preview should now smooth out into a 7-day rolling mean line.  
20. Verify the tooltip by hovering over any point—confirm it reads “AVG(Cost) – Rolling Mean (7)” and values are smoothed.  
21. Click the green “SAVE” button in the upper-right corner of the page.  
22. Type rolling_mean into the “Chart name” text box of the save dialog.  
23. (Optional) Choose or create the appropriate “Dashboard” or folder if you want to store it somewhere specific.  
24. Click the green “SAVE” button at the bottom of the dialog; you are redirected back to the Charts gallery.  
25. Confirm success by locating “rolling_mean” in the list—its Type column should read “Line chart” and clicking it should show the smoothed trend line with a legend entry “AVG(Cost) – Rolling Mean (7).”
