1. Click the left-side navigation item “Data Products”, then click the sub-menu “Marketplace”.  
2. Type “worldwide addresses” into the Search bar at the top of the Marketplace page and press Enter.  
3. Click the first search result titled “Worldwide Address Data (OpenAddresses)”.  
4. On the right-hand details pane, click the blue “Get” button located under the text “Free • Unlimited access”.  
5. In the pop-up window, click the small “Options” link to reveal additional settings.  
6. Highlight the default database name, type WORLDWIDE_ADDRESSES, and press Tab to confirm the change. (We rename it so our analysts can remember it easily.)  
7. Click the blue “Get” button at the bottom of the pop-up and wait until the status displays “Ready to use” (usually < 30 seconds).  
8. Click “Done” to close the window; the Marketplace page now shows a green badge “Added”.  
9. Switch to the top-right drop-down “Worksheets” (Snowsight automatically opens a new tab).  
10. Type the following verification commands in the worksheet:  
```sql
USE ROLE accountadmin;
USE DATABASE WORLDWIDE_ADDRESSES;
SHOW SCHEMAS;
```  
11. Click the ► Run button. Confirm the output includes multiple schemas such as “MAIN” or “PUBLIC”; this proves the dataset was imported successfully.
