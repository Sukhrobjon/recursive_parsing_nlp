1. Click the + (Add Sheet) icon at the bottom-left of the workbook to insert a new worksheet tab.  
2. Double-click the new tab name (likely “Sheet2”) to highlight it, type Sheet2, and press Enter to confirm the rename.  
3. Click the Sheet2 tab to make sure we are working inside this sheet.  
4. Drag the mouse from cell A1 to cell C1 so that A1:C1 are highlighted (three adjacent columns in the first row).  
5. Click the Merge cells button (it may look like two arrows converging) on the top-bar toolbar, then choose Merge & Center if a dropdown appears.  
   Note that Excel/Sheets will display “A1:C1” in the Name Box, confirming the merge.  
6. Type Investment Summary while the merged A1 cell is still selected, then press Enter; the text auto-centers across the merged range.  
7. Drag from cell A2 to cell B2 to highlight A2:B2 (the first two columns in the second row).  
8. Click the Merge cells button again and confirm Merge & Center.  
9. Type High Interest Rate in the merged A2 cell and press Enter.  
10. Drag from cell C2 to cell D2 to highlight C2:D2 (third and fourth columns in row 2).  
11. Click the Merge cells button once more and choose Merge & Center.  
12. Type Low Interest Rate in the merged C2 cell and press Enter.  
13. Click any blank cell (e.g., E3) to deselect the merged ranges and visually verify that:  
    • A1:C1 shows “Investment Summary”.  
    • A2:B2 shows “High Interest Rate”.  
    • C2:D2 shows “Low Interest Rate”.  
    All three labels should appear centered within their respective merged regions.
