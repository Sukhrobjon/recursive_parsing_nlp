1. Click the blue “New” button on the Metabase top-right corner.  
2. Select “Question” from the drop-down panel.  
3. Click “Raw data” ➜ choose “Sample Database” ➜ click the “Products” table.  
4. Click the teal “Visualize” button on the bottom-right; we’re now in the query builder.  
5. Click the “Summarize” button (upper-right of the screen).  
6. Click the “X” beside the default “Count of rows” chip to remove it.  
7. Click “+ Add” under Summarize by ➜ choose “Sum” ➜ pick the field “Price”; a chip reading “Sum of Price” appears.  
8. Click “+ Add” under Group by ➜ pick “Category”; a chip “Category” appears.  
9. Click “+ Add” again under Group by ➜ pick “Created At → Quarter”; Metabase shows “Created At – Quarter” as the second grouping chip.  
10. Click the blue “Done” button; the results preview now shows a pivoted table with each Category and its quarterly Sum of Price totals.  
11. Click the “Visualization” button (bottom-center bar) to open the visual sidebar.  
12. Click the “Bar” icon; Metabase immediately shows a clustered bar chart.  
13. Toggle the “Stacked” switch (located directly under the Bar chart options) so that the bars stack by Category for each quarter.  
14. Optionally verify: hover over one quarter segment; we can see tooltips showing each Category’s Sum of Price, confirming the aggregation is correct.  
15. Click the blue “Done” button in the sidebar to save the visualization settings.  
16. Click the cloud-shaped “Download” icon on the bottom-right corner of the screen.  
17. Select “PNG” in the “Download as…” dialog ➜ click “Download”. Metabase saves the file to your browser’s default download folder (usually “~/Downloads”) with a name like “Products_by_Category_and_Quarter.png”.  
18. Open your file browser and navigate to “~/Downloads”.  
19. Right-click the downloaded PNG ➜ choose “Rename” ➜ type stack_chart.png ➜ press Enter.  
20. (Optional) Drag stack_chart.png to your Desktop or desired folder for easy access.  
21. Double-click stack_chart.png to confirm the stacked bar chart renders correctly; we can see quarterly columns with colored Category segments — task complete.
