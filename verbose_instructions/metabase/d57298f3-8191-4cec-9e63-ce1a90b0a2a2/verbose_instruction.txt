1. Click your avatar (or initials) in the top-right corner of the Metabase window.  
2. Click “Admin settings” in the dropdown panel.  
3. Click “Databases” in the left-hand sidebar of the Admin page.  
4. Scan the list for the row named “Sample Database”.  
5. Click the blue “…” (three-dot) button at the far right of that “Sample Database” row.  
6. Click “Delete” in the small menu that appears.  
7. Type DELETE SAMPLE DATABASE in the confirmation dialog’s text box exactly as shown. (Note that Metabase asks for the database name in all caps to avoid mistakes.)  
8. Click the red “Delete database” button to confirm.  
9. Wait until the success toast (“Database deleted”) appears in the lower-left corner.  
10. Press F5 (or click the browser refresh icon) to reload the Databases page and verify that “Sample Database” no longer appears in the list.  
11. Click the Metabase logo in the top-left to return to the main homepage—your databases panel should now be empty.
