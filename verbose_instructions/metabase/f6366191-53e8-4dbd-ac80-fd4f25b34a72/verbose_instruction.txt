1. Click the ⚙️ “Settings” icon in the upper-right corner of Metabase.  
2. Choose “Admin” from the drop-down menu.  
3. Navigate to the left sidebar and click “People”.  
4. Click the “Groups” tab at the top of the screen (next to “People”).  
5. Click the blue “Add a group” button.  
6. Type Project Users into the “Group name” field.  
7. (Optional) Type “Regular users for the new project” in the “Description” box so future admins know the purpose.  
8. Click the blue “Save” button.  
9. Verify the new group appears in the groups list with the name “Project Users”. We will use this group in the next steps.

10. Click the “People” tab to return to the list of users.  
11. Type John Wilson into the “Search people” box in the upper-right, then press Enter.  
12. Click John Wilson’s name in the results to open the user detail pane.  
13. Click the “Edit groups” button (located in the “Groups” section on the right side).  
14. Check the box next to Project Users.  
15. Leave any existing group memberships as they are, then click “Save” in the “Edit groups” dialog.  
16. Confirm that “Project Users” now appears under the “Groups” heading on John Wilson’s profile.  

17. Switch to the “Groups” tab again, click Project Users, and confirm John Wilson is listed in the “Members” area—verifying that the assignment succeeded.
