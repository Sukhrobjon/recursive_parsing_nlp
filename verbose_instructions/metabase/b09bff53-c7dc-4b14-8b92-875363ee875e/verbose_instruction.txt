1. Click the grey gear icon in the upper-right corner of the Metabase web UI  
2. Select “Admin settings” from the drop-down menu that appears  
3. In the Admin panel, click “Databases” in the vertical navigation bar on the left (the list icon with stacked disks)  
4. Click the blue “Add a database” button on the upper-right of the Databases page  
5. Choose “PostgreSQL” from the “Database type” drop-down list (Metabase auto-loads the PostgreSQL-specific form fields)  
6. Type `PostgresTomDB` into the “Display name” input box  
7. Enter `localhost` into the “Host” field  
8. Type `5432` in the “Port” field (Metabase may auto-populate; overwrite if necessary)  
9. In the “Database name” box, type `TomDB` exactly as shown in db_info.json  
10. Enter `Tom` into the “Username” field  
11. Type `Tom123456` into the “Password” field and press TAB so Metabase masks the value  
12. Leave optional SSL and tunneling fields at default (unchecked) because db_info.json does not specify them  
13. Click the blue “Save” button at the bottom-right of the form  
14. Wait until the green toast notification “Successfully added PostgresTomDB” appears in the lower-left corner  
15. Confirm the new row labeled “PostgresTomDB” now shows in the Databases list with a status dot that is green (connected) – this verifies Metabase can reach the PostgreSQL instance
