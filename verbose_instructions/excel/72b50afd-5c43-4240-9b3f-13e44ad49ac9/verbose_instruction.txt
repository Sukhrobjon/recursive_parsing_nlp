1. Click cell G1  
2. Type the text “Revenue” and press ENTER so the column header is stored  
3. Click cell G2  
4. Type the formula  
```excel
=VLOOKUP(C2,'Retail Price'!$A$2:$B$23,2,FALSE)*E2*(1-F2)
```  
   and press ENTER to calculate the net revenue for the first record (Retail Price × Quantity × (1 – Discount))  
5. Drag the small square “fill handle” at the bottom-right corner of G2 straight down to row 36, then release the mouse so the formula fills G2:G36  
6. Verify that the last filled cell (e.g., G36) shows a numeric result rather than “#N/A”; if an error appears, re-check the VLOOKUP range and discount column references before proceeding  
7. Click any one cell in the header row (row 1)  
8. Press CTRL+SHIFT+→ then CTRL+SHIFT+↓ to highlight columns C through G for all occupied rows (this selects Product, …, Revenue)  
9. Click the “Insert” tab on the Ribbon, then click the “PivotTable” button in the Tables group  
10. Ensure the radio button “Select a table or range” is chosen and the selection box shows something like Sheet1!$C$1:$G$36; leave “New Worksheet” activated and click OK  
11. Switch to the new worksheet that Excel adds (it will be named “Sheet1!PivotTable1” or similar)  
12. In the PivotTable Fields pane, drag the field named “Product” into the Rows area  
13. Drag the field named “Revenue” into the Values area; Excel automatically sets it to “Sum of Revenue” (this is what we need)  
14. Confirm that the PivotTable now lists each distinct Product in column A and the corresponding Sum of Revenue in column B; totals should match a manual SUM of G2:G36  
15. Right-click the sheet tab currently titled something like “Sheet1 (2)” or “Sheet1!PivotTable1” at the bottom  
16. Choose “Rename” from the context menu  
17. Type “Sheet2” and press ENTER to finalize the new sheet name  
18. Press CTRL+S to save the workbook so the Revenue column and PivotTable summary are stored
