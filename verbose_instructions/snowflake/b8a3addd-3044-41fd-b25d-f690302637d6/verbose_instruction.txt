1. Click the “Worksheets” icon (sheet of paper) in the left-hand navigation bar so the Worksheets pane becomes active.  
2. Click the blue “+ Folder” button located at the top-left of the Worksheets pane.  
3. Type Online shopping in the Folder Name field that appears.  
4. Press Enter or click the blue “Create” button; we can now see an empty folder named Online shopping under “Private”.  
5. Hover over the new Online shopping folder name in the tree and click the three-dot (···) menu that appears to confirm we can see “New Worksheet” in the menu—this verifies the folder was created successfully.  
6. Click the Online shopping folder once to highlight it (this makes sure new worksheets are placed inside this folder).  
7. Click the blue “+ Worksheet” button at the top-left of the Worksheets pane.  
8. Type Books products in the Worksheet Name field of the popup.  
9. Make sure the Location dropdown shows “/Online shopping”; then click the blue “Create” button. A new blank worksheet tab titled Books products opens, and in the left pane we now see the worksheet listed under Online shopping.  
10. Click back on the Online shopping folder in the left pane to keep it selected.  
11. Click the blue “+ Worksheet” button again.  
12. Type Electronics products in the Worksheet Name field.  
13. Verify the Location still shows “/Online shopping” and click “Create”. The second worksheet tab Electronics products appears, and it is also listed in the folder.  
14. Look at the folder tree: we should see  
   • Online shopping  
     • Books products  
     • Electronics products  
   confirming both worksheets reside inside the new folder.
