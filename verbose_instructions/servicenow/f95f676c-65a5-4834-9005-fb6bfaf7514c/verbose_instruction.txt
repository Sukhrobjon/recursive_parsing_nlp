1. Open your preferred web browser and enter the URL for the Contract application (e.g., https://app.company.com/contract) in the address bar, then press Enter.  
2. Type your username in the “Email” field, enter your password in the “Password” field, and click the blue “Sign in” button.  
3. Wait until the Contract application’s main dashboard loads, and confirm you can see the left-hand navigation sidebar.  
4. Hover your mouse over the sidebar and scroll if necessary until the “Contracts” parent menu item is visible.  
5. Click the “Contracts” label (or its folder/chevron icon) once to expand its sub-menu options. You should now see entries such as “Leases,” “Licenses,” and “Agreements.”  
6. Click the “Leases” sub-menu item directly under “Contracts.”  
7. Verify that the page header now reads “Leases” and the URL ends with /contracts/leases (or similar), confirming you are in the “Contracts > Leases” module.
