1. Open your browser tab that is running Astronomer and wait until the Airflow UI loads the DAGs home page.  
2. Scan the table under the header “DAGs” and identify the first row in the list (this is the DAG with the smallest alphabetical order or earliest load time).  
3. Click the blue-colored DAG name hyperlink in that first row; Airflow redirects to the “DAG Details” screen for that project.  
4. Locate the horizontal menu bar inside the large content panel (tabs labeled “Graph”, “Tree”, “Grid”, etc.) directly under the DAG title.  
5. Click the tab labeled “Graph”.  
6. Wait a second for the DAG nodes and edges to render; confirm you can now see your tasks laid out as boxes connected by arrows.  
7. Hover over two or three nodes to verify their tooltips pop up—this confirms the view is interactive and fully loaded.
