1) Log in to dbt Cloud, locate the Projects list on the left sidebar, and click the project name “test_connection”.  
2) Click the blue Connect data button (label may read “Set up a connection”) that appears in the main panel.  
3) In the Choose a connection modal, click the BigQuery tile once so it gets a blue outline.  
4) Click the Next button in the lower-right corner of the modal.  
5) Wait a second; the modal expands into the Configure your environment view. Under the Settings subtitle, click the gray Upload a Service Account JSON file button (the button has an upward-pointing arrow icon).  
6) When your operating system’s file-picker window opens, navigate to Desktop in the left sidebar of the dialog.  
7) Double-click the dbt-user-creds.json file to select and upload it. We can see the filename appear just below the button once it’s accepted.  
8) Scroll down if needed and click the green Test connection button.  
9) Watch the banner at the top of the modal; it should turn green and display “Connection test succeeded.” (If it turns red, re-check that you selected the correct JSON key file.)  
10) Click the blue Next button to save this BigQuery connection to the “test_connection” project.  
11) Observe that the modal closes and the Connection tile on the project overview now shows BigQuery with a green checkmark, confirming the environment is ready.
