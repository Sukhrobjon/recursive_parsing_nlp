1. Click “Settings” (gear icon, top-right of Superset UI).  
2. Select “Database Connections” from the drop-down.  
3. Type “SpiderDatabase” into the search bar located above the database list so we can quickly isolate that entry.  
4. Click the blue pencil icon on the “SpiderDatabase” row to open the Edit Database modal.  
5. Scroll down inside the modal until you see the toggle labeled “Allow data upload”.  
6. Click the toggle so it turns blue (enabled).  
7. Scroll further down (if needed) and click the “Save” button at the bottom-right of the modal.  
8. Wait for the green toast message “Database updated” to confirm the change succeeded.  
9. Click “Data” in the top navigation bar.  
10. Choose “Upload a CSV” from the drop-down.  
11. Verify that the “Database” drop-down on the CSV Upload page now lists “SpiderDatabase”.  
12. If “SpiderDatabase” appears, the upload feature is enabled and you can proceed to actually upload CSV files later; if it does not appear, repeat steps 1-7 and ensure you clicked “Save”.
