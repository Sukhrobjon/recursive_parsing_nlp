1. Click the grey column header “A” above cell A1 to highlight the entire “Invoice No.” column.  
2. Click the “Insert” tab on the Ribbon, then click the “PivotTable…” button in the Tables group.  
3. In the “Create PivotTable” dialog, keep “Select a table or range” and “A:A” pre-filled, choose the radio button “New Worksheet”, and click the “OK” button.  
4. In the new PivotTable Field List pane on the right, drag the field “Invoice No.” down into the “Rows” box at the bottom-left.  
5. Drag the same field “Invoice No.” again into the “Values” box at the bottom-right.  
6. Observe that the entry now reads “Sum of Invoice No.”; click the small dropdown arrow to the right of this entry and choose “Value Field Settings…”.  
7. In the “Value Field Settings” dialog, select “Count” in the list of calculation types, then click “OK”.  
   Note that the value heading updates to “Count of Invoice No.”—this confirms we are counting occurrences rather than summing.  
8. Verify that the PivotTable in the worksheet now lists each unique Invoice No. in the left column with its corresponding count in the right column.  
9. Right-click the sheet tab labeled “Sheet1 (or similar)” created for the PivotTable and choose “Rename”.  
10. Type Sheet2 and press Enter to rename the worksheet.  
11. Press Ctrl + S to save the workbook so the new PivotTable and sheet name are retained.
