1. Open the browser tab that shows the target list (we will refer to it as the “Records” list; the exact table name appears in the upper-left corner, e.g., “Incident” or “Catalog Tasks”).  
2. Click the grey funnel icon labelled “Filter” in the list header to open the condition builder panel.  
3. Click the blue “New criteria row” (+) button once; a row with three dropdowns—Field, Operator, Value—appears.  
4. Select “Short description” from the Field dropdown.  
5. Select “is” from the Operator dropdown.  
6. Type Request to setup web conferencing into the Value text box and press Enter.  
7. Click the “OR” link that appears to the right of the row (this turns the next row into an OR condition group).  
8. Click the blue “New criteria row” (+) again to add a second row.  
9. Select “Type” in the Field dropdown of the second row.  
10. Select “is” in the Operator dropdown.  
11. Type Task in the Value field and press Enter.  
12. Click the “OR” link at the end of the second row.  
13. Click the blue “New criteria row” (+) to add the third row.  
14. Choose “Active” in the Field dropdown.  
15. Choose “is” in the Operator dropdown (it auto-populates with a True/False selector).  
16. Pick false from the Value dropdown.  
17. Click the “OR” link at the end of this third row.  
18. Click the blue “New criteria row” (+) to add the fourth row.  
19. Pick “Category” from the Field dropdown.  
20. Keep “is” as the Operator.  
21. Enter Services in the Value field and press Enter.  
22. Click the “OR” link at the end of the fourth row.  
23. Click the blue “New criteria row” (+) to add the fifth and final row.  
24. Choose “Name” from the Field dropdown.  
25. Ensure the Operator reads “is”.  
26. Type Web Conferencing into the Value box and press Enter.  
27. Review the condition builder; you should now see five rows connected exclusively by the word OR, confirming that any single match will be returned.  
28. Click the blue “Run” button in the upper-right corner of the filter panel.  
29. Observe the list refresh; verify that every displayed record satisfies at least one of the five conditions (we can spot-check by scanning the Short description, Type, Active, Category, and Name columns).  
30. Click the “Save” dropdown beside the Run button and choose “Save as” if you want to store this filter; type WebConferencing_Or_Filter, click “Save”, and confirm it appears under the “Filters” menu for future reuse.
