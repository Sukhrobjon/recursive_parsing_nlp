1. Locate the Explorer panel on the left side of the BigQuery console.  
2. Click the triangle (▸) next to your GCP project name to expand its contents.  
3. Look for the dataset named customer_orders under the project; click the small grey triangle (▸) beside it so we can confirm its tables appear (this ensures we’re pointing at the correct dataset).  
4. Right-click the customer_orders dataset name (or click the three-dot kebab icon that appears when you hover over it).  
5. Select Delete from the context menu that pops up.  
6. In the “Delete dataset” dialog, carefully review the dataset ID (it should read project-id:customer_orders) to verify you are deleting the intended dataset.  
7. Type the word DELETE into the confirmation text box exactly as instructed.  
8. Click the red Delete dataset button to execute the drop operation.  
9. Wait until the success toast “Dataset deleted” appears in the lower-right corner; this confirms the operation finished successfully.  
10. Expand your project again in the Explorer panel to verify the customer_orders dataset no longer appears—this final check ensures the dataset has indeed been removed.
