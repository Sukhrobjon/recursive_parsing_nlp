1. Click the Chromium icon on the left dock of your Astronomer workspace to bring the Airflow web-UI tab to the foreground.  
2. Hover over the Browse menu in the dark-blue top navigation bar until the drop-down list appears.  
3. Click DAG Dependencies in that drop-down list.  
4. Wait a second for the DAG Dependencies screen to render; the page title “DAG Dependencies” should now be visible at the top-left of the content pane.  
5. Move your mouse over any DAG box in the graph; we can see incoming (up-stream) and outgoing (down-stream) arrows confirming the dependency relationships.  
6. Type a DAG name into the Filter DAGs text box at the upper-right (e.g., airflow_example) and press Enter to isolate the graph for that single DAG.  
7. Verify that only the selected DAG and its direct parents/children remain highlighted while unrelated DAGs fade to grey—this confirms the filter worked.  
8. Click the Refresh button (blue circular arrow icon) above the graph if you need to reload the latest dependencies after changing DAG code.  
9. Press the Esc key or click the Airflow logo to return to the main DAGs view when you are done reviewing dependencies.
