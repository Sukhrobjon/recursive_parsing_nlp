To switch an existing Postgres-to-Postgres connection from Full refresh | Overwrite to Full refresh | Append, follow the steps below.

1) Open your browser and Navigate to the Airbyte UI (typically http://localhost:8000).  
2) Click the left-hand “Connections” menu item so we can view all configured connections.  
3) Locate the Postgres→Postgres connection in the list (look at the “Source” and “Destination” columns for confirmation) and Click anywhere on that row to open the connection detail page.  
4) Observe the top navigation bar inside the connection; Click the “Replication” tab (it is next to “Status” and “Settings”).  
5) Scroll to the “Stream configuration” table; each row represents a table coming from the Postgres source.  
6) For the first stream in the list, Click the pencil-shaped “Edit” icon on the far right of the row.  
7) In the “Sync mode” dropdown that appears, leave the left dropdown as “Full refresh” and Click the right-hand dropdown (currently set to “Overwrite”).  
8) Select “Append” from the dropdown options.  
9) Click the blue “Save changes” button at the bottom of the modal to close it.  
10) Repeat steps 6-9 for every remaining stream whose destination sync mode still shows “Overwrite” (Airbyte does not change all streams automatically).  
11) After all rows display “Full refresh | Append”, Scroll back to the top of the page and Click the “Save & Continue” button (or “Save changes” depending on your Airbyte version).  
12) Verify success: in the stream table each row should now read “Full refresh | Append” under the “Sync mode” column, and Airbyte shows a green “Changes saved” toast in the bottom left corner.  
13) Click the “Sync now” button in the upper-right corner of the connection page to run an immediate sync with the new settings.  
14) Wait until the job status turns to “Succeeded” in the job log panel; we have confirmed the connection is using Full refresh | Append.
