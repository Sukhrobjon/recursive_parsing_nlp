1. Navigate to the opened browser tab that contains your BI tool and Click the top-level menu item labeled “Dataset”.  
2. Locate the row that contains the table name flights and Click directly on the flights link to open its dataset builder.  
3. Drag the Department field from the left-hand column (under the list of columns) into the DIMENSIONS drop zone in the center panel.  
4. Drag the cost field from the same left-hand column into the METRICS drop zone.  
5. Hover over the just-added cost pill in METRICS and Click the small AGGREGATE drop-down that appears.  
6. Select AVG from the drop-down list so we aggregate cost by average. (Note that the label on the pill should now read AVG(cost); we will use this later when reading the chart.)  
7. Click the blue CREATE CHART button located at the bottom of the builder pane.  
8. Wait until the preview renders on the right side; we can see a table with two columns: Department and AVG(cost).  
9. Click the SAVE button in the upper-right corner of the screen.  
10. Select Save as… in the small modal that appears.  
11. Type Mychart into the Chart Name field exactly as shown.  
12. Click the Save button on the modal to persist the visualization.  
13. Navigate back to the main navigation bar and Click Charts.  
14. Verify that a new thumbnail titled Mychart is visible in the list; this confirms the chart was saved successfully.
