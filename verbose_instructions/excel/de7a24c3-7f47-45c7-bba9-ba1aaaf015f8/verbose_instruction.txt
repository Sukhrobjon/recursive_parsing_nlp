1. Right-click any existing sheet tab and choose “Insert” → “Worksheet” to add a blank sheet.  
2. Type “Sheet2” in the sheet-name box and press ENTER so the new sheet is called exactly “Sheet2”.  
3. Click cell A1 on Sheet2.  
4. Hold SHIFT and click cell B1 to highlight both A1 and B1 together.  
5. Click the “Merge & Center” button on the Home tab ribbon (Alignment group) to merge these two cells.  
6. Type Summer sales ($) and press ENTER to store the headline. Confirm that the text is centered across the merged cell A1:B1.  
7. Switch to “Sheet1” by clicking its sheet tab.  
8. Press CTRL+* (or CTRL+A twice inside the data block) to select the whole data range that contains the sales information. Make sure the top row contains the field names such as “Product”, “Revenue”, etc.  
9. Click the “Insert” tab and then click “PivotTable” (first icon in the Tables group).  
10. In the “Create PivotTable” dialog, confirm “Select a table or range” is chosen and that the Table/Range box shows something like ‘Sheet1!$A$1:$D$500’.  
11. Choose “Existing worksheet”.  
12. Click the “Location” box, then click Sheet2’s tab, and finally click cell A3 (directly under the heading) to place the pivot table starting at A3. Press OK.  
13. In the PivotTable Field List pane on the right:  
    a. Drag the field “Product” into the Rows area.  
    b. Drag the field “Revenue” into the Values area. Excel should default to “Sum of Revenue”. If it shows “Count”, click the drop-down beside it → “Value Field Settings…” → choose “Sum” → OK.  
14. Verify that the pivot table now lists each product name in column A and the corresponding total revenue in column B. We will use these two columns for the chart.  
15. Click any single cell inside the newly created pivot table to keep the whole pivot selected.  
16. Press CTRL+A once to highlight the entire pivot table (don’t include the Grand Total row).  
17. Click the “Insert” tab → choose “Bar Chart” → under the 2-D Bar section select “Clustered Bar” (this provides horizontal bars). A chart will appear on Sheet2.  
18. Click inside the chart title box, type Revenue of Each Product, and press ENTER.  
19. Click the legend once and press DELETE to remove it (horizontal bars already have labels).  
20. Drag the chart to an open area beneath the pivot table (for example, around cell E3) so both the pivot and chart are visible together.  
21. Resize the chart’s corners so all product names are readable; ensure the bars are horizontal and properly aligned.  
22. Click outside the chart to deselect everything.  
23. Press CTRL+S to save the workbook, confirming that Sheet2 now contains:  
    • a merged-cell heading “Summer sales ($)” in A1:B1,  
    • a pivot table starting in A3 summarizing Sum of Revenue per Product, and  
    • a horizontal bar chart titled “Revenue of Each Product” with no legend.
