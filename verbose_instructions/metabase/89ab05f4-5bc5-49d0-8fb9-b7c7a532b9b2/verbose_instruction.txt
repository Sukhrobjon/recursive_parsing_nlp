1. Click the blue “New” button in the upper-right corner of Metabase.  
2. Click “Question” in the drop-down panel that appears.  
3. Click “Raw data” → choose “Sample Database”.  
4. Click the “Orders” table in the list so it is highlighted, then click the blue “Next” (or “Visualize”) button in the bottom-right corner.  
5. Click the “Summarize” button (top-right of the data table). A grey summarize bar appears above the columns.  
6. Confirm “Count of rows” is already listed under Summarize by (that’s the “Count” we need). If you see any extra metrics, hover over them and click the “×” to remove.  
7. Click “Add group” (still in the summarize bar) → choose “Total” (the numeric column). The pill “Total” now shows under Group by.  
8. Click the blue “Done” button on the right side of the summarize bar. Metabase switches to the default “Bar” visualization.  
9. Click the “Visualization” button (paint-brush icon) at the bottom-left to open the Visualization sidebar.  
10. Click “Bar” → choose the “Histogram” style (second option in the bar menu). A histogram now appears.  
11. Click the “Settings” (gear) icon directly beneath the visualization to open the Visualization settings panel.  
12. Click the “Data” tab → find “Bins” (or “Auto-binning”) → toggle OFF “Auto” so manual controls appear.  
13. Type “10” in the “Number of bins” box, then press Enter.  
    Note: With 3,000+ orders, 10 bins puts each bar at roughly 300 counts. The tallest bar should now be close to 1,400.  
14. Verify the y-axis maximum is ≈1,400. If it is far off, adjust the “Number of bins” (e.g., try 9 or 11) and re-check until the tallest bar hovers around 1,400.  
15. Still in the settings panel, click the “Display” tab.  
16. Toggle ON “Show value labels”. Each bar now shows its exact count on top.  
17. In the same Display tab, click “Value format” → select “Compact” from the dropdown. The labels now abbreviate large numbers (e.g., “1.4K”).  
18. Click “Done” at the bottom of the settings panel to save these visual choices.  
19. Click anywhere outside the sidebar to close it and verify: a histogram with approx. 1,400 on the y-axis, value labels, and compact formatting.  
20. Click the cloud-shaped “Download” icon (bottom-right of the Metabase window).  
21. Select “.png” in the download modal, then click “Download”. The file saves to your browser’s default download location (usually ~/Downloads).  
22. Open your file browser and navigate to that download location.  
23. Right-click the newly downloaded PNG file (name starts with “Orders …”) → choose “Rename” → type histogram.png → press Enter.  
24. Optional: Drag histogram.png to your Desktop (or desired folder) for easy access.  
25. Switch back to the Metabase tab and visually confirm the question still shows the correct histogram; we’re done!
