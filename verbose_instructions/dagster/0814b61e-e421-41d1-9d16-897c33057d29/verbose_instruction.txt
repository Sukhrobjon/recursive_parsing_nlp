1. Locate the browser tab that is already showing the Dagster UI; if it is not in view, Switch to the tab titled “Dagster – Assets”.  
2. Hover over the left sidebar and Click the “Assets” icon (diamond-shaped) so the asset lineage graph appears in the main panel.  
3. Scroll or pinch-zoom on the canvas until we can see the asset node labeled `top10_story_ids`—it should sit near the top center of the graph.  
4. Click directly on the `top10_story_ids` node; the node border will turn blue and a right-hand “Asset Details” drawer will slide open, confirming the selection.  
5. Verify in the header of the right drawer that only 1 asset is selected (look for the text “1 selected”); if any additional assets are highlighted, Ctrl+Click them to deselect so that only `top10_story_ids` remains blue.  
6. Click the “Materialize selected” button located in the upper-right corner of the lineage graph toolbar.  
7. In the confirmation modal that pops up, review that the list contains only `top10_story_ids`, then Click the green “Materialize” button.  
8. Wait while Dagster redirects to the “Runs” page; we can see a progress bar labeled with the new Run ID. When the status indicator turns from “STARTED” to the green “SUCCESS” badge, the materialization is complete.  
9. Click the breadcrumb “Assets” in the top nav bar, Click the `top10_story_ids` node again, and check the right-hand drawer: the “Last materialized” timestamp should now reflect the current time, confirming success.
