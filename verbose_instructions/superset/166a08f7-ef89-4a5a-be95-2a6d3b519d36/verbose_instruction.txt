1. Click the avatar–shaped icon labeled “Settings” in the upper-right corner of the Superset Web UI  
2. Select “Action Logs” from the drop-down menu that appears  
3. Click the blue “FILTER LIST” button in the Action Logs screen  
4. Select “USER” in the left-hand column of the pop-up filter dialog  
5. Click the “Select value” input box that appears on the right side of the dialog  
6. Choose “Superset Admin” from the drop-down list of users  
7. Click the green “Search” button at the bottom-right of the filter dialog to apply the filter  
8. Click the column header “dttm” once so that a small ↑ (upward arrow) icon appears, indicating ascending chronological order  
9. Confirm that the earliest timestamp now appears in the first data row and that subsequent rows increase in time—this verifies the list is sorted correctly for your leader
