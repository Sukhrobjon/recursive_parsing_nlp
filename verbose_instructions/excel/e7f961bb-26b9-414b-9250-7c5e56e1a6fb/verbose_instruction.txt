1. Locate the header cell that contains the word “Revenue” (for example, cell D1 shows “Revenue”).  
2. Click the column letter directly above that header (e.g., click the grey rectangle marked “D”) so the entire “Revenue” column is highlighted.  
3. Press Ctrl + C to copy the column, including its header.  A dashed moving border should now surround the entire column—this confirms it’s on the clipboard.  
4. Move your cursor to the bottom sheet-navigation bar and click the small “+” (New sheet) icon just to the left of “Sheet1”.  A fresh tab named “Sheet2” (or “SheetN” if more sheets exist) opens in the workbook.  
5. Click cell A1 in this new sheet so it becomes the active cell.  
6. Press Ctrl + V to paste.  The “Revenue” header should appear in A1 and the entire column’s data should fill downward below it.  
7. Verify the paste worked correctly by confirming the header reads “Revenue” in A1 and the row counts match the original column length.  
8. Press Ctrl + S to save the workbook, ensuring the new sheet with the copied “Revenue” column is stored.
