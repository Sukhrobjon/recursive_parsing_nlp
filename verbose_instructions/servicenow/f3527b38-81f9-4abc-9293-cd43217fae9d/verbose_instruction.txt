1. Open your preferred web browser and navigate to the hardware store’s online portal (e.g., “https://hardware-store.example.com”).  
2. Click the Search bar at the top-right corner of the homepage and type “Sales Laptop”; press Enter.  
3. Locate the product listing labeled exactly “Sales Laptop” in the search results and click the product title to open its detail page.  
4. Verify that the product page header shows “Sales Laptop” and scroll down to the “Configuration” or “Customize” panel.  
5. Click the Quantity field (usually a small numeric input box) and type 3 so that we order three identical units.  
6. Click the checkbox or toggle next to “Salesforce” under Additional Software to enable it (we need this later for CRM work).  
7. Click the checkbox next to “Zoom” to enable video-conferencing capability.  
8. Click the checkbox next to “Microsoft Office 365” to include the full Office suite; note that this will already cover Word, Excel, and Outlook.  
9. Scroll to the “Adobe” subsection and confirm that “Adobe Acrobat” is unchecked (we do not need it).  
10. Click the checkbox for “Adobe Photoshop” so graphic editing is available for the sales team.  
11. Click the checkbox beside “Microsoft PowerPoint” to ensure presentation software is included.  
12. Click the checkbox labeled “Siebel Client” in the CRM add-ons list so the laptops ship with Siebel preinstalled.  
13. Review the summary in the right-hand “Configuration Preview” panel and confirm it shows: Salesforce, Zoom, Microsoft Office 365, Adobe Photoshop, Microsoft PowerPoint, Siebel Client, and explicitly indicates Adobe Acrobat: Off.  
14. Click the blue “Add To Cart” button located just below the configuration section; wait for the confirmation pop-up that says “3 × Sales Laptop added to cart.”  
15. Click the Cart icon (shopping-cart symbol) in the top-right navigation bar to open the mini-cart, then click “View Cart & Checkout.”  
16. Verify in the Cart page that Quantity reads “3” and each laptop line item lists all selected software options.  
17. Click the “Proceed to Checkout” button (often green or blue) at the bottom-right of the Cart summary.  
18. Type your Shipping Address and Contact Information in the appropriate text boxes; double-check for typos since shipment depends on this.  
19. Choose your preferred Shipping Method (e.g., “Standard – 3-5 business days”) via the radio button list.  
20. Scroll to the Payment section, select your payment method (credit card, purchase order, or corporate account), and enter the required credentials.  
21. Review the Order Summary panel one last time to ensure the total reflects 3 units and the correct software bundle.  
22. Click the final “Place Order” button; wait for the confirmation screen displaying “Order #xxxx successfully submitted.”  
23. Click the “Download Receipt (PDF)” button on the confirmation screen and save the file as “Sales_Laptop_Order.pdf” to your Desktop for record-keeping.  
24. Open your email inbox and check for the automated order-confirmation email titled “Your Hardware Store Order #xxxx”; file this email in your “Purchases” folder for easy future reference.
