1. Move the mouse cursor to the profile area in the upper-right corner of the Astronomer Airflow Web UI until the drop-down menu with your account name appears  
2. Click the Log Out option in that drop-down menu to terminate the current session  
3. Watch the page automatically redirect to the Astronomer sign-in screen; confirm that the Username and Password fields are visible and your name no longer shows in the header to verify the logout was successful
