1. Click the Chromium (or default browser) icon on the left panel of your Astronomer workspace to open the Airflow web UI  
2. Wait until the Airflow homepage loads, then look at the DAGs table in the center of the screen  
3. Identify the first DAG in the list (the top-most row; Airflow sorts alphabetically by default). We will refer to its DAG ID exactly as displayed—e.g. “example_first_dag”  
4. Click the blue DAG ID link of that first DAG to open its DAG details page  
5. Observe the sub-tabs at the top of the details page (“Graph”, “Tree”, “Grid”, “Code”, etc.)  
6. Click the “Graph” tab to switch to graph view  
7. Verify that a node-and-edge diagram appears; confirm task boxes are connected as expected and that the page title includes “Graph View”  
8. If the diagram is too large, use the mouse wheel to zoom out and drag the canvas until all tasks are fully visible—this confirms the DAG has rendered correctly
