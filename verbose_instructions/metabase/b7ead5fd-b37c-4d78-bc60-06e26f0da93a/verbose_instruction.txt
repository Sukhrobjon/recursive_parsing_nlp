1. Open your web browser and go to the Metabase login page (e.g., https://your-metabase-domain.com).  
2. Click the link labeled “Forgot password?” located beneath the blue Log in button.  
3. Type the email address associated with your Metabase account into the “Email” input box.  
4. Click the purple “Send reset link” button. Metabase displays “Email sent!” and sends a reset email to your inbox.  
5. Switch to your email client (Gmail, Outlook, etc.) and open the new message with subject “Reset your Metabase password.”  
6. Click the “Reset your password” button inside the email; a new browser tab opens with a secure reset form.  
7. Type metabase123 into the “New password” field.  
8. Re-type metabase123 into the “Confirm new password” field to ensure both entries match.  
9. Click the teal “Reset password” button. A green success banner appears stating “Password reset successfully.”  
10. Click “Return to login,” then enter your email and the new password metabase123 and press Enter.  
11. Confirm you are redirected to the Metabase home page (Questions / Dashboards list). This verifies the reset succeeded.  
12. Log out (click your avatar → Log out) and log back in once more with metabase123 to double-check everything is working properly.
