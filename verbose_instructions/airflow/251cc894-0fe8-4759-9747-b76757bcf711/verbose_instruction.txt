1. Click the Chromium icon on the left dock to bring the Airflow web-UI tab to the foreground.  
2. Verify that the page title reads “Airflow” and that you can see the main “DAGs” table; if not, press F5 to refresh once.  
3. Locate any row whose Owner column shows the blue text “Astro”.  
4. Click the blue “Astro” hyperlink inside that Owner cell—Airflow immediately filters the list so that only DAGs owned by Astro remain.  
5. Confirm the filter worked: the gray pill-shaped indicator Owner=Astro now appears above the table header.  
6. Move your cursor to the table header row and click once on the column title “DAG” (it may read “DAG Id” on some themes).  
7. Observe the small upward-pointing triangle that appears next to “DAG”; this indicates the list is now sorted in ascending (A→Z) order by dag_id.  
8. Read through the filtered list—every dag_id shown should now be alphabetically ordered.  
9. (Optional) Click “DAG” again if you need to toggle to descending and back to ascending to double-check; make sure you end with the upward triangle visible.  
10. Take note of the first and last dag_id in the list to prove the sort order (e.g., “astro_daily_report” … “astro_weekly_summary”).  
11. When finished, leave the Owner=Astro filter pill in place or click the small “×” inside the pill to clear the filter and return to the full DAG catalog.
