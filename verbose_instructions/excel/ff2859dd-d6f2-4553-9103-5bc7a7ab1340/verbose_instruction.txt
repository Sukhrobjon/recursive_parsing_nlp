1. Click cell A12 directly beneath the last product row.  
2. Type Total and press Enter so the row header is recorded.  
3. Click cell B12 (the first numeric cell in the new “Total” row).  
4. Type =SUM(B2:B11) into the Formula Bar and press Enter; Excel immediately shows the column total.  
5. Verify that the value in B12 is non-zero and roughly matches an expected monthly sum (this confirms the formula worked).  
6. Hover over the small square “fill handle” in the bottom-right corner of B12 until the cursor becomes a black plus sign.  
7. Drag the fill handle horizontally from B12 across to G12, then release the mouse; Excel autofills totals for all remaining months.  
8. Confirm that each cell C12:G12 now contains a SUM formula referencing its own column (e.g., in C12 you should see =SUM(C2:C11) in the Formula Bar).  
9. Hold Ctrl and click cell A12 to include the “Total” label in the upcoming chart selection.  
10. While still holding Ctrl, drag across B12:G12 so the entire total data range A12:G12 is highlighted.  
11. Continue holding Ctrl, then drag across B1:G1 to add the month headings to the selection (your combined selection should now be A12:G12 plus B1:G1).  
12. Release Ctrl so both header row and total row remain highlighted.  
13. Click the Insert tab on the Ribbon.  
14. Click the Insert Line or Area Chart icon (it looks like a jagged line).  
15. In the drop-down gallery, click the first option under “2-D Line” labeled Line (Excel calls this the “Lines Only” style).  
16. Observe that a new line chart appears on the worksheet; months populate the horizontal axis and totals form a single series.  
17. Click anywhere on the blank chart title placeholder to activate edit mode.  
18. Type Total and press Enter so the chart title updates.  
19. Click once on the legend (it likely shows “Series1”) to select it.  
20. Press Delete to remove the legend, satisfying the “no legend” requirement.  
21. Review the chart: only one polyline is visible, the title reads “Total,” and no legend is displayed.  
22. Click an empty cell to deselect everything and save the workbook with Ctrl+S.  
23. Re-open or refresh the worksheet to verify the Total row still shows formulas and the chart still reflects your data.
