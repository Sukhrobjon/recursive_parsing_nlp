1. Click any single cell inside your data range (for example, A1) so Excel can automatically detect the full block (columns A-D).  
2. Press Ctrl + A to ensure every populated row in columns A through D is highlighted.  
3. Click the Insert tab on the Ribbon.  
4. Click PivotTable (first icon in the Tables group).  
5. Verify the “Table/Range” box correctly shows something like $A$1:$D$### (where ### is your last row).  
6. Select the radio button “New Worksheet” so the pivot will be placed on a separate sheet.  
7. Click OK. Excel jumps to “Sheet1!PivotTable1” (or a similar new sheet).  
8. Drag the field “Expense Account” from the PivotTable Fields pane into the Rows area (lower-left quadrant).  
9. Drag the field “Subtotal” into the Values area (lower-right quadrant). Excel automatically applies “Sum of Subtotal.”  
10. Confirm that each unique Expense Account now appears once, with a numeric total beside it, and a “Grand Total” row is visible at the bottom.  
11. Click anywhere inside the new PivotTable to keep it selected for charting.  
12. Click the PivotTable Analyze tab (or just Analyze in older versions) that appears on the Ribbon.  
13. Click PivotChart (in the Tools group).  
14. Choose “Bar” on the left list, then select the first subtype “Clustered Bar” in the right pane.  
15. Click OK. Excel inserts a bar chart on the same pivot-sheet.  
16. Click the chart title once, type “Subtotal by Expense Account”, and press Enter.  
17. Right-click any legend entry and choose Delete to remove the legend (all bars already labeled by the vertical axis).  
18. Drag the chart if necessary so it doesn’t overlap the PivotTable; release the mouse when properly placed.  
19. Verify the chart bars align with the PivotTable numbers: the height of the “Grand Total” bar should equal the Grand Total figure, and individual bars must match each row’s subtotal.  
20. Press Ctrl + S.  
21. In the “Save As” dialog, ensure “Excel Workbook (*.xlsx)” is selected (this is the 2007-365 format), choose your desired folder, type a descriptive file name like “Expense_Account_Subtotals.xlsx”, and click Save.  
22. Reopen the saved file to confirm the pivot table and bar chart both appear and function correctly.
