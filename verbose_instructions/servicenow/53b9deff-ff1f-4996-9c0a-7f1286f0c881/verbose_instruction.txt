1. Locate the page title “servicenow” near the top-left; directly beneath it, Click the middle icon that looks like a Funnel to open the Filter & Sort panel.  
2. Wait until the panel titled “Filter” and “Order results by the following fields” is fully loaded (we should see an “Add Sort” button).  
3. Click the blue “Add Sort” button once to create the first sort rule row.  
4. In the newly added row, Click the dropdown immediately under the text “Order results by the following fields”.  
5. Type “Display name” in the type-ahead search box and Press Enter to select “Display name” from the list.  
6. Move to the dropdown just to the right of “Display name”; Click it and Select “z to a” so the arrow points downward (this sets Descending order).  
7. Click “Add Sort” again to append a second rule row.  
8. In the second row’s field selector, Click the dropdown, Type “Cost center”, then Press Enter to pick “Cost center”.  
9. Click the adjacent ordering dropdown for the second row and Select “a to z” (Ascending).  
10. Click “Add Sort” a third time to create the final rule row.  
11. In the third row, Click the field selector, Type “Stockroom”, and Press Enter to choose “Stockroom”.  
12. Click the ordering dropdown next to “Stockroom” and Select “a to z” (Ascending).  
13. Review all three rows: we should read  
   • Display name – z to a  
   • Cost center – a to z  
   • Stockroom – a to z  
   If any row is incorrect, Click its field or order dropdown to adjust.  
14. Click the blue “Run” button at the bottom-right of the panel to apply the sort rules and refresh the hardware list.  
15. Verify the list: Confirm that the first column “Display name” is sorted in reverse alphabetical order; within any identical display names, confirm the “Cost center” column shows alphabetically ascending values, and within those, the “Stockroom” column is also ascending. If the order is incorrect, Repeat steps 1-14 to correct the settings.
