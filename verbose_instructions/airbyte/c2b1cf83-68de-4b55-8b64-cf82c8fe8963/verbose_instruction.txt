1) Switch to the Airbyte browser tab that is already open  
2) Click the blue link that shows the Postgres ➜ Postgres connection name in the Connections list (it is on the right-hand side of the screen)  
3) Click the Replication button (third tab in the horizontal menu bar)  
4) Scroll to the table titled “Activate the streams you want to sync”; in the Sync mode column click each dropdown that currently shows “Full refresh | Overwrite”  
5) Select “Full refresh | Append” from the dropdown for every stream (you will see the pill update to Append for each row)  
6) Click the blue Save changes button in the bottom-right corner of the page  
7) On the confirmation dialog that appears, click Save connection to persist the new sync mode  
8) Wait until the green “Changes saved” toast appears in the lower left corner to verify the update succeeded
