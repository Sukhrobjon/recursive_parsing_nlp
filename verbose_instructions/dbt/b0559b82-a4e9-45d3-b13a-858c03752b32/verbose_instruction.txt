1. Click the Deploy button located in the dark-blue top navigation bar.  
2. Select Jobs from the Deploy drop-down list that appears.  
3. Click the blue Create job button in the upper-right corner of the Jobs page.  
4. Select Deploy job from the small pop-up menu (this opens the New job configuration screen).  
5. Type Sales_Summary into the Job name field under the Job settings panel on the left (you should see the name appear in the breadcrumb at the top—this confirms it registered).  
6. Scroll down to the Triggers section and toggle on the option labeled Run when another job finishes (the trigger sub-form expands once enabled).  
7. Click the Project drop-down inside that sub-form and choose Christmas from the list of available projects (we need the project that owns the upstream job).  
8. Click the Job drop-down directly beneath it and select Sales_Process (this is the upstream job that must finish first).  
9. In the Completes on chips that appear, click Success so that only Success remains highlighted (make sure Failure and Error are not selected—only green Success should stay lit).  
10. Verify that the grey helper text now reads “Runs after Sales_Process (Success)”—this confirms the dependency is configured correctly.  
11. Scroll back to the top of the page while keeping an eye on the blue bar; it should now show “Dirty” indicating unsaved changes.  
12. Click the Save button in the upper-right corner (the button label switches to Saved and the Dirty indicator disappears).  
13. Observe the toast notification “Job Sales_Summary saved” in the lower-left corner—this is our confirmation that the job configuration was persisted.  
14. Navigate back to the main Jobs list by clicking the Jobs breadcrumb; ensure Sales_Summary now appears with “Triggered by: Sales_Process (Success)” in the Trigger column.
