1) Navigate to the Google Cloud console in your browser and Click the left-hand menu item “BigQuery” so the BigQuery Explorer panel loads.

2) Verify you are in the correct GCP project by looking at the project selector in the top bar; if it is not the intended project, Click the project name dropdown and Choose the correct project.

3) In the Explorer pane (left side), Hover over the line that shows your project ID until the grey “+ ADD” button appears, then Click “+ ADD”.

4) Observe that a slide-out panel titled “Add data” opens on the right. In this panel, Click the card labeled “Connections to external data sources”.

5) Wait for the “Create connection” dialog to load, then Scroll if needed until you see the group “Vertex AI remote models, remote functions and BigLake (Cloud Resource)” under “Connection type”.

6) Click the radio button in front of “Vertex AI remote models, remote functions and BigLake (Cloud Resource)” so it becomes highlighted.

7) Move to the “Connection ID” field that appears below, Type exactly:
```
text_generation
```

8) Optionally, review the “Location” dropdown; leave it at its default (usually “US”) unless you need a specific region for your Vertex AI resources.

9) Click the blue button labeled “CREATE CONNECTION” at the bottom of the panel.

10) Wait until the toast notification “Connection created” appears in the lower-right corner; this confirms the connection was provisioned successfully.

11) Verify the new connection: in the Explorer pane, Expand the “External connections” section under your project and Confirm you can see an entry named “text_generation” with the Vertex AI icon.
