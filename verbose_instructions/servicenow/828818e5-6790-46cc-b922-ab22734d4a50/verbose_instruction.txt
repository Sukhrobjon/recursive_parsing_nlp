1. Open the ServiceNow instance in your browser and wait until the home page finishes loading.  
2. Click the global filter at the very top-left, type “Change” in the search box, and press Enter.  
3. Locate the module “Create New” under the “Change” application in the left navigation pane and click it; a blank Change Request form appears.  
4. Click the input field labeled Short description (it is the first text box near the top) and type exactly:  
   Switch Sales over to the new 555 prefix  
5. Click directly into the large multi-line box labeled Description (immediately below Short description) and paste the full text, preserving all internal spaces:  
   Sales has their own private dialing prefix now so they'll            always have an open outside line even if the PBX is full.  
6. Click the input field labeled Number (directly under Description). Delete any auto-generated value and type:  
   CHG0000002  
7. Click the drop-down field labeled Category to the right of Number. Scroll (if necessary) and select the option: Telecom  
8. Click the drop-down field labeled Risk (found just below Category) and choose: High  
9. Click the drop-down field labeled Impact (immediately under Risk) and confirm or pick: 3 - Low  
10. Verify that the Service field (above Category) remains empty; leave it blank as required.  
11. Click the tab named Planning in the horizontal tab strip beneath the Basic Information section; the Planning form pane becomes visible.  
12. Scroll to the Justification text area (first field in this pane) and type:  
    unbigged didelphoid gigantology unimprovableness fistula  
13. Click the next text area labeled Implementation plan and type:  
    unfrocked pedunculus tensive pharmacodynamic immunology  
14. Click the text area labeled Backout plan (below Implementation plan) and enter:  
    nonproportional reassurance inchoateness pericementum wandoo  
15. Click the final text area in the pane labeled Test plan and enter:  
    strette cupriferous polylinguist unambitious Ganodonta  
16. Confirm that the Service offering field in the left column of Basic Information is blank; do not populate it.  
17. Review every filled field for accuracy, then click the blue Submit button at the bottom-left of the form. The page refreshes back to the Change list with no error, indicating the new Change Request has been created successfully.
