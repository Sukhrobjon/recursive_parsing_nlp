1. Click the Chromium icon in the left-hand dock to switch to the Airflow web UI opened by Astronomer.  
2. Wait until the Airflow homepage loads and we can see the list of DAGs under the “DAGs” tab.  
3. Locate your target DAG (for example, type the first few letters into the Search box if the list is long) and verify that its row is visible.  
4. Click the blue DAG name itself (not the toggle switch) to navigate into that DAG’s detail page.  
5. Confirm we are in the DAG details page by checking the breadcrumb in the upper-left corner that now reads something like “DAGs / your_dag_id”.  
6. Look at the horizontal view selector directly under the DAG title; the options are “Grid”, “Graph”, “Calendar”, “Task Duration”, “Gantt”, “Landing Times”, and “Dependencies”.  
7. Click the “Dependencies” tab (it is usually the last option on the right).  
8. Observe the DAG Dependencies view that appears:  
   • Upstream DAGs (those that trigger this DAG) are shown on the left in blue boxes.  
   • The current DAG is centered in a grey box.  
   • Downstream DAGs (those triggered by this DAG) appear on the right in green boxes.  
9. Hover over any DAG box to highlight its edges; notice that Airflow shows arrows pointing from upstream → current → downstream, confirming the dependency chain.  
10. Verify that the dependency arrows correspond to what you configured in your code (e.g., using TriggerDagRunOperator or dataset-based scheduling).  
11. (Optional) Click any upstream or downstream DAG box; Airflow opens that DAG’s detail page in the same tab so we can further inspect its own dependencies.  
12. When finished, click the “DAGs” breadcrumb at the top to return to the main list or simply close the tab.

That’s it—this sequence lets us view and confirm DAG-level dependencies directly in the Airflow web UI deployed on Astronomer.
