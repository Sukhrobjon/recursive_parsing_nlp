1. Click the top-level menu item “Charts” to open the chart list page  
2. Click the blue “+ CHART” button in the upper-right corner of the screen  
3. Click inside the “Choose a dataset” search box that appears in the modal dialog  
4. Type “game_sales” and press Enter, then click the returned line labeled “game_sales” to select it  
5. Look to the panel on the right side of the modal, scroll if needed, and click the card labeled “Line Chart”  
6. Click the blue “CREATE NEW CHART” button at the bottom of the modal to open the chart builder  
7. Drag the field “year” from the left-hand column list into the “X-AXIS” drop zone in the control panel  
8. Drag the field “global_sales” from the same column list into the “METRICS” drop zone  
9. Click the small gray dropdown inside the “global_sales” metric pill that currently says “SUM” or “COUNT”  
10. Select “AVG” from the aggregation list so the pill now reads “AVG(global_sales)”  
11. Click the blue “RUN” button above the chart canvas to render the preview; confirm the line plot shows one point per year and the Y-axis is “AVG(global_sales)”  
12. Click the green “SAVE” button at the top-right of the screen  
13. Type “linechart” into the “CHART NAME” input field in the save dialog  
14. Click the blue “SAVE” button in the dialog to persist the visualization  
15. Observe the toast notification “Chart linechart was saved” in the lower-right corner, confirming success and adding the chart to the main chart list
