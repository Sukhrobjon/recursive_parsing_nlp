1) Switch to the browser window that is already displaying your local Airbyte UI (typically http://localhost:8000).

2) Click the link “Sources” in the dark-blue left sidebar.

3) Type “Faker” in the search bar at the top-right of the “Sources” page; we can see a tile named “Sample Data (Faker)” appear.

4) Click the tile “Sample Data (Faker)”.

5) Click the blue button “Set up source” in the upper-right corner of the page.

6) Wait a few seconds until the green badge “All tests passed” is shown under “Status”; this tells us the source is valid.

7) Click the link “Destinations” in the left sidebar.

8) Type “local json” in the destination search bar; only one card called “Local JSON” should remain visible.

9) Click the card “Local JSON”.

10) In the popup form, locate the input labeled “Destination Path” and type exactly:
/local/json_destination

11) Click the blue button “Set up destination” at the bottom of the form.

12) Wait until the green badge “All tests passed” confirms the destination has connected successfully.

13) Click the link “Connections” in the left sidebar.

14) Click the large button “Create your first connection” (or “New connection” if other connections exist) in the main panel.

15) In the “Define source” step, click the dropdown “Create a new source” and choose “Select an existing source”.

16) In the list that appears, click the row for “Sample Data (Faker)” (we created it seconds ago).

17) Click the blue button “Next” in the bottom-right corner.

18) In the “Define destination” step, click the dropdown “Create a new destination” and choose “Select an existing destination”.

19) Click the row “Local JSON” in the list, then press “Next”.

20) In the “Configure connection” step, scroll to the “Streams” table; locate the “products” stream and click the toggle in its “Status” column so it displays an open green icon.

21) For every other stream (e.g., customers, orders, payments, users), click the same toggle so each shows a closed grey icon. Only “products” should remain enabled.

22) Find the “Sync frequency” section on the right sidebar, click the dropdown that currently reads “Every hour” and change it to “Manual”.

23) Quickly review that:
   • “products” status = Enabled  
   • All other streams = Disabled  
   • Sync mode = Manual  

24) Click the blue button “Set up connection” in the lower-right corner.

25) Wait for the toast “Connection successfully created” and confirm we land on a new connection overview page that shows “Manual sync” under “Schedule”.

26) (Optional verification) Click “Sync now” once to run an immediate test; the status bar should turn blue (“Running”) and then green (“Succeeded”). A JSON file will appear under /local/json_destination/products_date_time.json confirming end-to-end success.
